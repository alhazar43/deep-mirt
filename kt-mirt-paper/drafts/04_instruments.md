# 4. Instruments, twins, and beds

This section names the instruments under audit, the twins and bank they run against, and the nine triaged real beds; Sections 5 and 6 then execute this stated design.

Four instruments enter the protocol. The passive existence gate, the growth arm's primary readout, compares a growth model of pooled learner-KC slices against a fitted no-growth reference under the permutation machinery of Section 3. The active posture (ACT) fits an explicit per-KC growth channel and reads magnitude and rank from its parameters. A no-decay pin (rho fixed at 1) structurally bars it from representing an individual learner's decline; decline-side slices are read by passive machinery only, the scope row recorded in Section 3 [G21]. The association arm's transfer readout estimates a signed cross-KC coefficient matrix; its per-learner multiplier is pinned at gamma=1, since a free or amortized multiplier fabricated transfer under every estimation posture previously tested [S17]. The state transition is response-blind, because responses feeding it sign-reversed transfer coefficients on sparse edges and fabricated them on null data [S18]. The cross-KC route is structurally isolated and unit-tested; with the matrix zeroed, a non-practiced KC's state never leaves its initialization [S19]. The fourth instrument is not ours. PAS-N1, a field-representative shared-state neural tracker in the recurrent design deep KT defaults to, enters as an object under audit, not a baseline to beat. Its per-KC-factorized variant PAS-N2 makes the audited confusions impossible by construction, but that is a guarantee, not a measured verdict; no faithfulness-audit cell exists for it anywhere, only a same-ballpark predictive sanity probe [P2].

One precondition binds every parametric instrument. Both pre-convergence ACT reads were optimization artifacts in opposite directions, a fabricated rise of 0.246 on the no-growth twin (25 times the silence bar) and a spurious silence on true growth, traced to the default optimizer stopping orders of magnitude early; a stationarity-gated trainer recovers 0.154 against a true 0.15, and growth-channel gates are read only under it [G22]. The no-growth twin caught a defect prediction metrics never would, the protocol auditing its own optimizer. The same flat-basin pathology reappears in the transfer setting (Section 6) [S6].

The synthetic twins are calibrated to two profiles at opposite ends of the density range, a few-deep KDD-shaped profile and a many-thin EdNet-shaped profile (189 KCs, 6,000 learners, median 2 opportunities per pair, tag arity 2.2) [M2]. Each profile carries four twins, a no-growth null, two growth twins of different shape, and a saturated twin placing genuine growth near ceiling. Every twin runs at five slice seeds; neural arms run three model seeds on one generator seed, a pre-registered compute concession [M7]. Extreme step sparsity forces the difficulty bank into a hierarchy. With 1,313,276 distinct steps at median frequency 1 (74.3 percent singletons), a free per-step difficulty is unidentified, so step offsets are fit only at adequate calibration counts; the EdNet-shaped bank is dense enough for a single level on the same code path [E13]. All per-slice fits are penalized, since quasi-complete separation is routine at these slice lengths, with the penalty shared identically by null, alternative, and permutation replicates [E14]. The bank's recovery verdict is a standing condition. Difficulty rank correlation sits at 0.70 to 0.80 against the pre-registered 0.9 bar, a bank-fidelity floor that rides into every downstream readout using the bank [G12].

Nine real beds were triaged through one shared metric library (Table 1). No bed wins every axis, a split that is itself a finding, so bed choice is per avenue [R15]. KDD Algebra is the primary growth bed, 515 KCs and 335,430 learner-KC slices at median 8 opportunities, with growth verdicts running on the unsaturated 48.3 percent of KCs [R14]. At 82 to 86 percent correct it is also the most saturated growth-bearing bed [R7], yet model-free first-attempt curves rise 12 to 19 points over ten opportunities, genuine growth escaping the aggregate ceiling [R5]. Junyi 2020 is the designed thin-practice contrast, the standout bed for individual trajectory depth yet with a shallow pooled trend, median slope about a quarter of KDD's [R10]; its single-parent topic tree yields zero KC co-occurrence, structurally barring cross-skill work under its shipped KC scheme [S26]. EdNet KT1's feared saturation was a mislabeled model-accuracy figure; the raw task is comfortably unsaturated, but median-2 density restricts it to population-level reads [R8]. It is capped at Tier 1 regardless of results, since bundle exposure co-schedules KCs, fatal to causal reads, and ACT never runs on real EdNet [R9]. Eedi leads decoupling at 0.967 and is the only bed with a real negative-transfer hook, genuine wrong-answer concentration without option-position bias; misconception labels do not ship with its logs (the 2020 release carries none) [S22], and its growth signal is the weakest of the nine [R11]. junyi15 at topic grain is the strongest growth-magnitude bed, rising 0.626 to 0.815 over ten opportunities, with an unresolved within-topic exercise-sequencing confound that could inflate the curve [R6]. TIMSS, a single-occasion static-calibration bed with growth structurally undefined [R12], and Duolingo SLAM, which ships no KC tags, are excluded from the growth program [R13].

The KC model is measured, not assumed; on KDD, KTracedSkills clears decoupling where SubSkills fails (0.800 against 0.267 on top-30 pairs) and avoids a catch-all non-content tag that inflates Rules, a profile Bridge replicates [S24]. Decoupling is the fraction of co-occurring KC-pair slots in which exactly one member is observed, with a 0.75 admission bar (detail in Appendix B) [M13]. Against that bar EdNet at 0.87 and Eedi at 0.967 pass, while Junyi's exercise-grain decoupling is unmeasured, gating the external-validation leg of the association arm [S25].

A final caution, the program's single most important methodological one, shapes every later pair selection. Volume-ranked top-K selection can be adversarially unrepresentative, since volume and universality are mechanically correlated for foundational content; junyi15's top-30 prerequisite pairs clear decoupling 6.7 percent of the time while the full pair population clears 72.2 percent [M9]. Every later top-K read is therefore checked against its full pair population first.
