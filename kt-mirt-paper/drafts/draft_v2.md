# A Certification Protocol and Licensing Map for the Latent-State Readouts of Multi-Skill Knowledge Tracing [working title]

## Abstract

Deep knowledge-tracing models are increasingly read as measurement instruments, their latent states published as growth curves and cross-skill influence graphs. The field validates these readouts by prediction accuracy, which cannot certify them. We present a certification protocol that decides, before any readout is interpreted, whether the estimator recovers known truth. Its instruments are synthetic twins matched to named real datasets, pre-registered bars and stopping criteria frozen before any run, permutation nulls, designed confound arms, and end-to-end seed discipline. Refusals are published as results, each with its mechanism. In the one completed cycle, a passive growth-existence detector is certified at cohort grain on twin profiles at both density extremes, and its one diagnosed false fire, on near-ceiling data, is repaired with a saturation-aware null. The certified instrument then fires on real KDD Algebra and stays correctly silent on thin-practice Junyi, a cross-bed contrast consistent with a data-density boundary whose deciding cell is still running. A second instantiation, caught mid-cycle and reported that way, yields to our knowledge the first certified signed cross-KC readout. Its own controls demote it to per-edge signed dose-association, certified on the synthetic harness at a single named operating point, under an idealized calibrated bank the program's own bank does not reach. The deliverable is a licensing map labeling every audited readout certified, confirmed, refused with mechanism, or pending. The refused cells include per-KC growth resolution at both density extremes, growth-magnitude corridors, and the field-default neural tracker's faithfulness battery on the synthetic twins. The map states which interpretive sentences these readouts license and where they stop.

## 1. Introduction

Deep knowledge-tracing (KT) models are routinely read as measurement instruments. Their latent states are published as growth curves, per-skill mastery, and cross-skill influence graphs. The nearest published neighbors, Deep-IRT (Yeung 2019), PSI-KT (Zhou et al. 2024), HawkesKT (Wang et al. 2021), and LTKT (Xu et al. 2025), validate those readouts by prediction accuracy alone, never by testing whether the estimator recovers known structure. Both claims this paper targets, signed influence recovery and a trustworthiness audit of KT readouts, are to our knowledge unclaimed in prior work (Section 2) [S21].

Prediction cannot certify a readout. Raw learning curves cannot either; they are attrition-biased, since late-practice learners are not a random sample of those who started (Nixon et al. 2018). Two results in Section 5 sharpen the point. The clean raw-statistic separation between growth and no-growth data on a deep-practice profile is partly a density artifact, gone at thin practice [G3]. And a gate with a misspecified no-growth reference fires confidently on near-ceiling data, a false fire raw curves cannot diagnose [G5].

A companion paper prices the stakes on the item side (Anonymous, under review). Auditing static, single-skill KT models, it found item-parameter readouts can be stable and wrong. Accuracy, rerun stability, and split-half reliability all pass while the shared readout channel's item parameters err badly against ground truth. In its simulations the decision cost was a roughly doubled adaptive-test length and 2.3 percentage points of added cut-score misclassification. Even where item maps agreed, person-side summaries remained path-sensitive, a warning aimed at exactly this territory. This paper takes up what that audit disclaims, person-side dynamics and structure across multiple knowledge components (KCs). The cost of a misread growth curve or influence graph is argued from that precedent, not measured here.

Our answer is a certification protocol that asks, before any readout is read, whether the estimator recovers known truth [M1]. Its instruments are matched synthetic twins calibrated to named real datasets, with the target effect switched on or off by construction, bracketing two density extremes [M2]. Around them sit pre-registered bars and kill conditions frozen before any run [M3], permutation nulls [M6], designed confound arms, and end-to-end seed discipline [M5]. Refusals are published with the same standing as positive results. A readout is certified when it passes those bars on the synthetic harness under seed discipline; Section 3 gives the word its exact, limited meaning. The protocol runs end to end once and transfers once.

This paper makes four contributions.

1. **A certification protocol for the latent-state readouts of multi-skill KT models.** Matched twins, frozen bars and kill conditions, permutation nulls, confound arms, seed discipline, refusals as deliverables [M1, M2, M3, M4, M8]. The ingredients are established practice (Section 2); the claimed novelty is the assembly for this object and its outputs, not the epistemic move.

2. **A certified growth-existence readout with one closed certify-then-confirm loop.** The passive gate certifies at cohort grain on both density-extreme twin profiles, silent on every null twin and at the permutation floor on every growth twin, reporting presence, never size [G1, G2, G4]. Its one diagnosed failure, a false fire on the saturated twin, is traced to reference-model misspecification and repaired with a saturation-aware null [G5, G6, G8]. The repaired instrument fires on real KDD Algebra under that null, on the unsaturated KC subset (257 of 515), at one seed and one cohort split, with p at the B=99 floor of 0.01. It stays correctly silent on thin-practice Junyi. The contrast is consistent with a data-density boundary of the method rather than an absence of learning, and the cell that would settle the reading is still running [R1, R2, P1]. Model-free curves already rise on KDD, so the fire validates the instrument rather than discovering growth [R5]; the arm rolls up as partially certified [R3].

3. **To our knowledge the first certified signed cross-KC readout, demoted by its own controls to dose-association at a named operating point.** Signs are recovered from the fitted model near truth [S2]. On the synthetic harness (D=3 KCs, N=500 learners, single-tag density, calibrated bank, untuned default trainer), the readout earns exactly one licensed sentence, per-edge signed dose-association, with a measured negative-edge detectable-dose floor of 0.04, twice the field-anchored negative reference dose of 0.02 [S16, S11]. The calibrated-bank condition is idealized; the program's own bank recovers below its bar, and the paper measures how the floor moves under density and bank error [S7, S8]. A measured false-edge background mandates multiplicity control on any real-data use [S12]. A phantom-transfer control passes [S13], an order-shuffle control refutes the temporal reading [S14], and the causal reading is left unearned pending a pre-registered scheduling control [P5]. The remaining battery is pre-registered and unexecuted [P3].

4. **The licensing map, with refusals as first-class, mechanism-attached results.** Per-KC growth resolution fails at both density extremes with the same failure signature [G9]. Growth magnitude and per-KC ranking fail their corridors [G13]. The field-representative shared-state neural tracker fails the faithfulness battery on both synthetic twin profiles at production scale [G17]. The association sign collapses at multi-tag density [S7], and decline is out of scope by construction [G21]. Every audited cell is labeled certified on the synthetic harness, confirmed on real data, refused with mechanism, or pending pre-registered; the deliverable is the map plus the harness to re-check it (Table 5).

The architecture is asymmetric by design. The growth arm completes one certify-then-confirm cycle; the association arm is the protocol's second instantiation, caught mid-cycle and reported as far as it got. The certificates attach to the paper's own instruments, the passive gate and a deliberately constrained transfer readout (Section 4). The field-default tracker we test is refused, a result Section 5 delivers [G17]. Sections 2 through 4 set out lineage, protocol, and instruments; Sections 5 and 6 execute the two arms; Section 7 assembles the map; Sections 8 and 9 weigh what transfers and what it costs. Nothing here improves prediction, and no such claim is made. The aim is narrower, to say which interpretive sentences these readouts support and where support ends.

## 2. Related work

Four strands organize prior work. Each names what is established and what remains ours.

### 2.1 Uncertified readout consumption in knowledge tracing

Deep KT models increasingly ship latent-state readouts presented as measurement. Deep-IRT attaches an IRT output layer to a memory network and presents the resulting ability and difficulty estimates as explainable by construction (Yeung 2019). PSI-KT claims interpretability by design for its learner traits and inferred prerequisite graph (Zhou et al. 2024). The graph line extracts inter-skill structure directly, from the influence graph of the original DKT (Piech et al. 2015) through GKT (Nakagawa et al. 2019) and GIKT (Yang et al. 2020). In every case the validation offered is predictive, an AUC gain or a face-valid inspection, never a test of whether the estimator recovers known structure.

Two neighbors touch signed cross-skill structure and require exact placement. HawkesKT models temporal cross-effects between skills through mutual excitation (Wang et al. 2021). Its cross-effect parameters are signed, but they are uncertified and response-conditioned. The signs are interpreted for prerequisite discovery, and the negative direction arises when a wrong answer on one skill depresses the prediction on another, not as a skill-to-skill interference estimate. Its sole external check is ranking agreement with expert annotators on a collapsed positive-only score (NDCG 0.83) [S21]. LTKT models concurrent positive and negative learning transfer among concepts, and we credit it for that framing (Xu et al. 2025). Its signed graph, however, is a stipulated input. The signs come from a hand-designed co-occurrence heuristic, frozen before training and computed over training, validation, and test data jointly, a leakage the paper itself states. Its validation is predictive ablation only. Neither work asks whether its estimator recovers known signs, and neither reports a detection floor. Our association claim therefore rests on a three-way conjunction. The signs are recovered from the fitted model, certified against ground truth with designed confound arms, and accompanied by a harness-measured detectable-dose floor.

The 2025-26 newcomers continue the pattern. KeenKT, UKT, and PAKT enrich the readout itself, with distributional mastery states (Li et al. 2025), uncertainty-aware embeddings (Cheng et al. 2025), and an ability-proficiency decomposition (PAKT 2026), and KTCF generates counterfactual recourse from KT models (Kim, Lee, and Kim 2026); all validate by prediction benchmarks. The audit-flavored newcomers stop short of the latent state. They re-evaluate experimental protocols (Kim and Kim 2026), audit deployment behavior beyond AUC (Yamkovenko et al. 2025), measure robustness under concept drift (Lee et al. 2025), or ask whether interpretable KT changes teacher decisions (Khalid, Deriyeva, and Paassen 2025). The closest neighbor in spirit corrects per-item bias in frozen KT models through empirical-Bayes shrinkage (Yan, Tang, and Shimada 2026). That line repairs prediction logits on real data, without ground truth and without certification; we certify readouts before any claim is read and refuse those that fail. A verification sweep found both of this paper's target claims, signed influence recovery and a trustworthiness audit of KT readouts, genuinely open [S21].

### 2.2 Growth detection and its known limits

Detecting learning in log data is prior art. The P(J) detector estimates the probability that a student learned a skill at a specific step (Baker, Goldstein, and Heffernan 2011). Learning-factors analysis fits per-skill learning-rate slopes with standard inference (Cen, Koedinger, and Junker 2006), and iAFM extends slope inference at scale (Koedinger et al. 2023). None of this is certified against ground truth, and the learning curves it rests on can be artifacts of attrition (Nixon et al. 2018). The limits are equally well documented. Observed performance is compatible with families of parameter estimates that predict identically yet make different knowledge claims (Beck and Chang 2007; van de Sande 2013; Doroudi and Brunskill 2017). The predictive advantage of deep KT is largely explained without per-skill tracking (Khajah, Lindsey, and Mozer 2016), and wheel-spinning, extended practice that never reaches mastery, bounds what near-mastered data can reveal (Beck and Gong 2013). We cite this literature ahead of our results deliberately. When the protocol later refuses per-KC resolution or diagnoses a false fire under saturation, the content of those verdicts echoes these ancestors; what the protocol adds is a certified, dataset-specific verdict with the mechanism attached. Detection and slope inference are prior art. Certification is not.

### 2.3 Methodological lineage

The protocol's ingredients have owners, and we cite them before a reviewer does. Simulation-based calibration validates inference machinery on data simulated from the model before trusting real-data inferences (Talts et al. 2018). Randomization-based sanity checks showed that widely used interpretability readouts fail null tests they should pass (Adebayo et al. 2018). Permutation nulls and negative-control designs are standard practice in statistics and epidemiology (Lipsitch et al. 2010). Minimum detectable effects have two lineages we draw on, the psychometric power literature on detecting change (Hertzog et al. 2006; Rast and Hofer 2014) and the minimum-detectable-effect tradition imported into NLP benchmarking (Card et al. 2020). Parameter recovery on synthetic data is routine in KT itself (Lan et al. 2014), and a recent pre-fit diagnostic asks whether neural interaction discoveries are recoverable at all, the same epistemic move in a different domain (Kuskova, Zaytsev, and Coppedge 2026). What we claim is not the epistemic move. It is the assembly for KT latent-state readouts, and one device within it, the matched synthetic twin. A twin is a replica calibrated to a named real dataset with the readout's target switched on and off, bracketing the density range with profiles at both extremes [M2]. None of the cited lines constructs that object or gates real-data claims on its verdicts.

### 2.4 Change measurement and the validity argument

Measuring change is an old psychometric problem, with a formal tradition of structured models for growth (Andersen, 1985; Embretson, 1991; Fischer, 1995; McArdle, 2009). The reliability of difference scores has a long-running debate of its own, opened by the classic pessimism of Cronbach and Furby (1970) and answered by Rogosa and Willett (1983), who showed difference scores are often highly reliable. The power to detect change, and individual differences in change, has been quantified in that tradition for decades (Hertzog et al. 2006). We concede the test forms here are classical. The permutation gate, the corridor bars, and the floor measurements assemble standard machinery. What we take from validity theory is the frame. Kane's interpretation and use arguments treat validation as an explicit chain of inferences (Kane, 2013), each of which must be licensed before the interpretation it supports is used. The protocol operationalizes that program for the readouts of deep KT models. Every cell of the licensing map is such an inference, licensed with conditions or refused with a mechanism, and a refusal is an explicitly unlicensed inference published as a result rather than buried, in the register of Mayo's severe testing (Mayo, 2018). This is not offered as a contribution to validity theory. The contribution is the working protocol and the map it produces for the readouts a fielded KT model publishes.

## 3. The certification protocol

The protocol enforces one discipline, certify before claim. No latent-state readout is interpreted until the same estimator has recovered known truth on synthetic data, and the real-data phase of each arm begins only once a synthetic certificate licenses it [M1]. Throughout this paper the word certified has an operational meaning. A readout is certified when it passes pre-registered bars on the synthetic harness under seed discipline, with every threshold frozen before any run. Certification is necessary, not sufficient. It licenses a claim against the failure modes the harness was built to expose, and it guarantees nothing under failure modes the harness does not contain. Where the campaign's own records disagreed on the strength of a verdict, the weaker reading governs, with one adjudicated exception disclosed in a footnote where it arises (Section 6.3). The protocol's ingredients are individually established practice. Validating inference machinery on data simulated from a known model is the logic of simulation-based calibration (Talts et al., 2018). Randomization tests that a readout must pass before it is trusted are the sanity-check posture of interpretability auditing (Adebayo et al., 2018). Negative controls are standard in epidemiology and causal inference (Lipsitch et al., 2010). What we claim is the assembly of these instruments around the latent-state readouts of knowledge-tracing models, and the output states that assembly produces. Figure 1 sketches the pipeline.

The device that makes the assembly work is the matched twin, a synthetic replica calibrated to the interface statistics of a named real dataset, with growth or cross-skill transfer switched on or off by construction. A detector can then be run against datasets that differ only in whether the effect it reports actually exists. Two profiles at opposite density extremes, a few-deep profile and a many-thin profile, bracket the range occupied by the real beds, the real datasets the protocol runs against. A result holding at both ends is unlikely to be a quirk of one data shape, so no third profile was needed [M2]. We call the profiles KDD-shaped and EdNet-shaped. The names record calibration targets and never stand in for real-data results. The bracket supports robustness to density only. It says nothing about robustness to the generating process, since the twins share the harness's model family, a limit Section 5 probes from inside and the discussion returns to.

Both designs were pre-registered in full before any run. The growth design's registration (A4, v1.1, frozen 2026-07-18) fixed its estimands, generator, ten-arm battery, and thirteen certification gates ordered positive-control first, together with kill conditions K1 through K7, pre-registered failure criteria that terminate an arm and mandate a stated negative verdict. Thresholds may tighten but never loosen, and revisions were capped at two rounds [M3]. The transfer design's registration (A1, v1.1, frozen 2026-07-23) inherited the two-revision rule and added a power precondition, designed confound twins, a saturation-matched null, kill conditions K-T1 through K-T6, and a pre-registered clean-negative verdict [M4]. Both registrations reached their frozen versions before any run, and their full version histories, with timestamps, ship in the released evidence registry (see the availability statement). Every kill condition carries a named honest verdict, the registrations' term for the negative result that must be reported if the kill fires, so a dead end is documented rather than buried [M8]. Table 2 lists the gates and kills compactly with their frozen bars and executed verdicts; Appendix A holds the full registry, including the arms that remain pre-registered and unexecuted.

The statistical core is a permutation null built against fitted no-growth references, never against bare zero. The gate's decision quantity, the bed statistic quoted throughout Sections 5 and 7, is the bed-level aggregate over analysis slices of the fit advantage of the growth model over the fitted no-growth reference, a pooled model-comparison statistic. Positive values mean the growth model explains the response sequences better, negative values favor the no-growth reference, and the permutation replicates recompute the same quantity with the growth-bearing structure destroyed, the p-value being the observed statistic's rank among them. A fitted quantity carries a per-seed offset, so observed effects are compared with the 95th percentile of the same quantity fitted on no-effect twins, the matched-null band [M6]. Because the null is a fitted reference rather than a distribution-free construction, the test is size-valid only for the family of no-effect processes that reference spans. Certification is therefore relative to the reference model, a dependence the saturated twin later makes concrete (Section 5). Seed discipline runs end to end. Hyperparameter tuning used held-out seeds 100 through 102 and produced no winner, so the default trainer was never tuned and certification seeds 0 through 4 stayed clean [M5]. Confirmatory claims must be sign-consistent in every seed and significant when seeds are pooled; neural arms run three model seeds as a stated pre-registered compute concession [M7]. Multiplicity control is itself certified rather than assumed. The realized false-discovery rate of the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure is certified on the null twin at both densities, with the dependence-robust Benjamini-Yekutieli variant reported beside every discovery list, and that certificate is scoped to per-KC growth statistics only [M10].

Synthetic effect sizes are anchored to the field rather than chosen for convenience. Growth magnitudes and the two reference transfer doses, +0.05 facilitation and -0.02 interference, are set from the published population learning rate of 0.1 log-odds per opportunity (Koedinger et al., 2023). Per-KC rates are spread twenty-fold so that rank recovery is a meaningful target, and a dose-response sweep at 0.5, 1, 2, and 4 times the reference doses is pre-registered [M12]. The rate estimand is affine-invariant to any positive rescaling of the ability scale, so it cannot be biased by the scale gauge, the arbitrary origin and unit of the latent ability scale; displacements and asymptotes are gauge-dependent and are reported only on the frozen anchored scale [M11]. At the existence tier the growth design has exactly three independent inputs, and firing of the actively trained posture alone is never claimed as growth [M14]. Reference transfer edges are placed only between KC pairs sharing no co-tagged item, so synthetic sign recovery measures practice-gated transfer with temporal lag rather than co-observation. Same-slot co-observation is tested as a separate confound arm that must not be read as transfer [M15].

The certified object is named exactly. It is the existence of monotone ability gain at cohort grain. Decline is out of scope by construction. The active posture's no-decay pin structurally bars it from representing an individual learner's decline, and decline-side slices are read by passive machinery only [G21]. This recorded scope limit is the protocol's first refused row, entered before any experiment is reported, and it travels into the licensing map like every other verdict.

Finally, the protocol defines refusal as an output class rather than a caveat. Every audited readout cell ends in one of four states. Certified on the synthetic harness means the pre-registered bars passed under seed discipline. Real-data confirmed means a synthetically certified instrument then ran on a real bed and behaved as certified. Refused with mechanism means a bar failed and the failure was traced to a cause that is published with the verdict. Pending pre-registered means the arm is frozen but unexecuted. Sections 5 and 6 deliver verdicts in all four states, and Section 7 assembles them into the licensing map.

## 4. Instruments, twins, and beds

This section names the instruments under audit, the twins and bank they run against, and the nine triaged real beds; Sections 5 and 6 then execute this stated design.

Four instruments enter the protocol. The passive existence gate, the growth arm's primary readout, compares a growth model of pooled learner-KC slices against a fitted no-growth reference under the permutation machinery of Section 3. The active posture (ACT) fits an explicit per-KC growth channel and reads magnitude and rank from its parameters. A no-decay pin (rho fixed at 1) structurally bars it from representing an individual learner's decline; decline-side slices are read by passive machinery only, the scope row recorded in Section 3 [G21]. The association arm's transfer readout estimates a signed cross-KC coefficient matrix. Its per-learner multiplier is pinned at gamma=1, since a free or amortized multiplier fabricated transfer under every estimation posture previously tested [S17]. The state transition is response-blind, because responses feeding it sign-reversed transfer coefficients on sparse edges and fabricated them on null data [S18]. The cross-KC route is structurally isolated and unit-tested; with the matrix zeroed, a non-practiced KC's state never leaves its initialization [S19]. The fourth instrument is not ours. PAS-N1, a field-representative shared-state neural tracker in the recurrent design deep KT defaults to, enters as an object under audit, not a baseline to beat. Its per-KC-factorized variant PAS-N2 makes the audited confusions impossible by construction. That is a construction guarantee with no measured verdict; no faithfulness-audit cell exists for PAS-N2 anywhere, only a same-ballpark predictive sanity probe [P2].

One precondition binds every parametric instrument. Both pre-convergence ACT reads were optimization artifacts in opposite directions, a fabricated rise of 0.246 on the no-growth twin (25 times the silence bar) and a spurious silence on true growth. The cause was the default optimizer stopping orders of magnitude early. A stationarity-gated trainer recovers 0.154 against a true 0.15, and growth-channel gates are read only under it [G22]. The no-growth twin caught a defect prediction metrics would not have caught, the protocol auditing its own optimizer. The same flat-basin pathology reappears in the transfer setting (Section 6) [S6].

The synthetic twins are calibrated to two profiles at opposite ends of the density range, a few-deep KDD-shaped profile and a many-thin EdNet-shaped profile (189 KCs, 6,000 learners, median 2 opportunities per pair, tag arity 2.2) [M2]. Each profile carries four twins, a no-growth null, two growth twins of different shape (kg, known growth of the generator's standard shape; ns, non-standard-shape growth that the fitted family misfits), and a saturated twin placing genuine growth near ceiling. Every twin runs at five slice seeds; neural arms run three model seeds on one generator seed, a pre-registered compute concession [M7]. Extreme step sparsity forces the difficulty bank into a hierarchy. With 1,313,276 distinct steps at median frequency 1 (74.3 percent singletons), a free per-step difficulty is unidentified, so step offsets are fit only at adequate calibration counts; the EdNet-shaped bank is dense enough for a single level on the same code path [E13]. All per-slice fits are penalized, since quasi-complete separation is routine at these slice lengths, with the penalty shared identically by null, alternative, and permutation replicates [E14]. The bank's recovery verdict is a standing condition. Difficulty rank correlation sits at 0.70 to 0.80 against the pre-registered 0.9 bar, a bank-fidelity floor that rides into every downstream readout using the bank [G12].

Nine real beds, KDD Algebra, KDD Bridge to Algebra, EdNet KT1, Junyi 2020, junyi15, Eedi, XES3G5M, TIMSS, and Duolingo SLAM, were triaged through one shared metric library (Table 1). No bed wins every axis, a split that is itself a finding, so bed choice is per avenue [R15]. Decoupling, the triage's pair-separation metric, is the fraction of co-occurring KC-pair slots in which exactly one member is observed, with a 0.75 admission bar (detail in Appendix B) [M13]. KDD Algebra is the primary growth bed, 515 KCs and 335,430 learner-KC slices at median 8 opportunities, with growth verdicts running on the unsaturated 257 of 515 KCs [R14][R1]. At 82 to 86 percent correct it is also the most saturated growth-bearing bed [R7]. Yet model-free first-attempt curves rise 12 to 19 points over ten opportunities, genuine growth escaping the aggregate ceiling [R5]. Junyi 2020 is the designed thin-practice contrast, the standout bed for individual trajectory depth yet with a shallow pooled trend, median slope about a quarter of KDD's [R10]. Its single-parent topic tree yields zero KC co-occurrence, structurally barring cross-KC work under its shipped KC scheme [S26]. EdNet KT1's feared saturation was a mislabeled model-accuracy figure; the raw task is comfortably unsaturated, but median-2 density restricts it to population-level reads [R8]. EdNet is capped at Tier 1, population-grain existence claims only, regardless of results, since bundle exposure co-schedules KCs, fatal to causal reads; ACT never runs on real EdNet [R9]. Eedi leads decoupling at 0.967 and is the only bed with a real negative-transfer hook, genuine wrong-answer concentration without option-position bias. Misconception labels do not ship with its logs (the 2020 release carries none) [S22], and its growth signal is the weakest of the nine [R11]. junyi15 at topic grain is the strongest growth-magnitude bed, rising 0.626 to 0.815 over ten opportunities, with an unresolved within-topic exercise-sequencing confound that could inflate the curve [R6]. TIMSS, a single-occasion static-calibration bed with growth structurally undefined [R12], and Duolingo SLAM, which ships no KC tags, are excluded from the growth program [R13].

The KC model choice rests on measurement. On KDD, KTracedSkills clears decoupling where SubSkills fails (0.800 against 0.267 on top-30 pairs) and avoids a catch-all non-content tag that inflates Rules, a profile Bridge replicates [S24]. Against the 0.75 bar EdNet at 0.87 and Eedi at 0.967 pass, while Junyi's exercise-grain decoupling is unmeasured, gating the external-validation leg of the association arm [S25].

A final caution, the program's single most important methodological one, shapes every later pair selection. Volume-ranked top-K selection can be adversarially unrepresentative, since volume and universality are mechanically correlated for foundational content. junyi15's top-30 prerequisite pairs clear decoupling 6.7 percent of the time while the full pair population clears 72.2 percent [M9]. Every later top-K read is therefore checked against its full pair population first.

## 5. The growth-existence readout: a completed cycle

This section walks the growth-existence readout through the full cycle the protocol prescribes, certification on matched twins, one diagnosed false fire and its repair, and a real-data confirmation paired with a designed silence. The refusals at finer grain are not caveats to that arc. They are results of the same rank, delivered here with their mechanisms and their lineage.

### 5.1 Certification at cohort grain

The passive existence gate asks one question of a dataset, whether pooled ability gain is present at cohort grain, and answers it against a permutation null built from fitted no-growth references. Certification requires silence on the no-growth twin and detection on both growth twins, on both density profiles, under the frozen bars of Section 3.

The gate certifies on both counts. On the no-growth twin the permutation p-values stay dispersed and unremarkable, 0.128 to 0.973 on the KDD-shaped profile and 0.222 to 0.876 on the EdNet-shaped profile. Both growth twins are detected at the permutation floor of p = 0.001 in every seed on both profiles, with no overlap between the two distributions; one EdNet growth cell, ns seed 1, is absent from the artifact set, with no recorded reason [G1]. The verdict survives the full density inversion, holding unchanged when the profile flips from few-deep to many-thin. The null is clean at finer grain as well. Per-KC discovery counts under Benjamini-Hochberg (BH) control are zero in all five seeds on the thin profile, whose null twin is in fact tighter than the deep one, with split-half gaps of 0.042 to 0.051 against the KDD-shaped 0.066 to 0.098 [G2]. Figure 2 shows the certification matrix, four twins by five seeds by two profiles.

One result disciplines this headline. The visually clean separation of raw bed statistics on the deep profile, no-growth values at or below 915 against growth values at or above 2375, is partly a density artifact and is not sold as general. At thin density the raw bands overlap, four of five kg growth-twin statistics fall inside the no-growth range, and only the calibrated permutation p-value recovers the separation [G3]. The gate's portability across densities lives in the calibration, not in the raw statistic; the inset of Figure 2 shows the overlap.

A second cap follows from the permutation arithmetic. With 999 replicates the smallest attainable p-value is 1/(999+1) = 0.001, so every floor value quoted here is a bound, not a graded strength. The gate reports that growth is present and nothing about how much, and it cannot rank one detection above another once both sit at the floor [G4].

### 5.2 The saturation false fire, diagnosed and repaired

The gate's one diagnosed failure is the saturated twin. That twin carries a true ability rise byte-identical to the kg growth twin's, placed near ceiling where observed performance has little room left to move; the gate must stay silent there. Instead it inverts, firing at the permutation floor in every seed on both profiles, hardest exactly where silence is required [G5]. We read the inversion as the harness doing its intended work, probing the reference model's specification rather than the learners' dynamics.

The mechanism is misspecification near ceiling. The no-growth reference approximates a saturating curve worse than the growth model does, which hands the growth side a near-universal held-out likelihood edge regardless of the true dynamics [G6]. Two independent signals corroborate the diagnosis, a numerical degeneracy that pins the fitted rate uncertainty at the numerical floor imposed by a likelihood ridge, and a split-half reliability gap of 0.259 to 0.292 against a 0.10 tolerance that appears on the saturated twin only [G6]. The limits of near-mastered data are classical territory, with wheel-spinning its sharpest prior name (Beck & Gong, 2013). What the harness adds is the demonstration, against known truth, that a permutation gate with a misspecified reference converts a ceiling into a confident positive. Density modulates the severity of the false fire but not its existence, a bed-statistic inflation of 3.7x on the deep profile against 1.32x on the thin one [G7].

The repair follows the diagnosis rather than the symptom. A saturation-aware bed null excludes near-mastered KCs from the bed-level decision and touches nothing else; every per-KC statistic is byte-identical before and after. The repair eliminates the false fire in a verification rerun at B=99 replicates, whose smallest attainable p-value is 0.01. The saturated twin moves from the p=0.01 floor to 1.00, detection on both growth twins holds at the B=99 floor, and the no-growth null stays clean [G8]. Three cautions attach to the repair, stated here because the real-data step inherits them. The verification reran the twin set that exposed the failure; no fresh twin was generated to test the repair out of sample. The reduced replicate count is a compute concession to the permutation battery's cost at scale, and every p-value it produces is reported as the floor it is. And the masking rule was fixed with the repair, before any real-data run, but the sensitivity of a bed verdict to its threshold has not been measured. Figure 3 shows the inversion and the repaired matrix side by side. Every certification statement from here on is relative to the repaired reference, and the repair itself becomes a licensing rule, no growth claim on saturation-prone data without a saturation-aware null.

### 5.3 Refusals at finer grain

Below cohort grain the protocol mostly refuses, and the refusals carry some of the section's most consequential content.

Per-KC resolution leads. The gate that certifies cleanly at cohort grain licenses nothing one level down. Across all five seeds the per-KC discovery count under BH control is 0 of 515 on the deep profile and 0 of 189 on the thin one, by comparable margins, with the same failure signature, invariant under the full density inversion [G9]. The margins are wide. On the positive-control growth twin the minimum per-KC p-value is 0.005 against a BH threshold of 9.7e-5, so realized power at this grain is identically zero on both profiles. The second growth twin's discoveries are erratic on the deep profile (65, 0, 55, 0, 0 across seeds), absent on the thin one, and vanish everywhere under the dependence-robust Benjamini-Yekutieli variant [G10]. The pre-registered rate-recovery kill condition supplies the quantitative floor, a rank correlation of about 0.08 between fitted and true per-KC rates against a 0.6 bar at thin density [G11]. The same signature at both density extremes, at comparable margins, is what moves this from a sparsity complaint to an estimator-level verdict. We infer, labeling it as inference, that more data at these densities will not license per-KC growth claims. One arm that would harden the inference has not run; the design varies density, not sample size at fixed density, so a common sample-size threshold sitting below both densities is not excluded by measurement. The content of the refusal has ancestors. Per-skill non-identifiability has been on record since Beck and Chang (2007), and Khajah et al. (2016) showed that a deep tracker's advantage need not come from per-skill tracking. The deliverable here is the certified, harness-specific verdict with measured margins, not the rediscovery. Figure 4 collects the evidence.

A standing condition rides along with every readout that touches the bank. The frozen difficulty bank recovers at rank correlation 0.70-0.80 against its 0.90 bar, and this bank-fidelity floor travels into each downstream verdict, the growth gate included [G12].

The active posture (ACT) fares worse than the passive gate. It is refused as a magnitude estimator and as a per-KC ranker, undershooting true magnitude by roughly 5-10x, a magnitude ratio of 0.086 against the pre-registered 0.5-1.5 corridor, with per-KC rank correlations of 0.27-0.38 on the deep profile against a 0.5 bar [G13]. The refusal is not total. ACT does recover the presence and direction of growth on both profiles, and per-KC rank is markedly better at thin density, where one variant (act_p1) clears the relaxed positive-control bar at 0.599 [G14]. That discipline does not transfer. No ACT variant is clean on both the null and the growth twins across both profiles simultaneously. At thin density the null breaches on both variants, a 95th-percentile firing level of 0.040 against a 0.02 bar on one and a population level of 0.028 on the other, while the same variant is fully silent on the deep profile [G15]. The misfit clause intended to catch model breakdown is unusable on both profiles by opposite numeric routes, firing on 6-7% of growers where 80% is required on the deep profile and over-firing to non-informativeness on the thin one, where it flags 64-74% of growers but also 58-71% of the silent subset [G16]. Table 3 lays out the corridor, rank, and null-silence grid across variants and profiles.

Reliability closes the ladder. Per-learner split-half reliability, the agreement between gate statistics computed on the two halves of each learner's sequence, passes on the three non-saturated twins on both profiles, with deep-profile gaps of 0.066-0.098 inside the 0.10 tolerance. It is blown out only on the saturated twin, where the 0.259-0.292 gap doubles as a saturation diagnostic [G19]. One tier could not be tested at all. Per-KC rate reliability at the second reporting tier cannot be certified from any existing artifact, because the KC-level split-half leg is dead code, defined in the battery but never called by the runner. We report that as a harness wiring gap, not a statistical null [G20].

### 5.4 The field-default tracker fails the battery

The certificates above attach to the paper's own instruments. The architecture the field reads by default was put through the same battery and refused. PAS-N1, the field-representative shared-state neural tracker of Section 4, fails the faithfulness battery consistently on both synthetic twin profiles at production scale, across all four audits, the frozen-encoder null, the drill-contamination probe, the order-invariance check, and the direction and reconstruction audit. On the deep profile the first three audits pass 0 of 12 cells each and the reconstruction audit fails 11 of 12, with violation magnitudes of 0.21-0.47; the thin profile repeats the pattern at 0 of 3 per twin, with only partial order-invariance passes [G17]. The scope is the synthetic twins, and we state it as such. PAS-N1 ran under the same battery and seed regime as the paper's own instruments, at three model seeds, the pre-registered neural compute concession [M7]. It received no diagnose-and-repair cycle of the kind the paper's own instruments received, and the asymmetry is deliberate. The paper's instruments carry the paper's claims, so their diagnosed defects had to be repaired before certification; the tracker enters as the audited object, tested as the field configures and reads it. Its repair path exists and is named, the per-KC-factorized PAS-N2, and it stays unmeasured rather than presumed [P2]. One borderline cell is recorded, a single reconstruction pass at 0.028 against the 0.10 tolerance on one saturated-twin seed, a genuine exception inside an otherwise-uniform sweep that changes no twin-level verdict [G18].

The decoupled variant PAS-N2 earns no symmetrical sentence. Its audit superiority is a construction guarantee, not a measured result. No faithfulness-battery verdict exists for it in any cell on either profile, and the only number attached to it is a same-ballpark held-out likelihood probe, 0.4326 against PAS-N1's 0.4446 [P2]. The paper claims nothing for it beyond the construction. Table 4 gives the cell-by-cell battery.

### 5.5 Real-data confirmation and designed silence

The synthetic roll-up licenses the real-data step and bounds it. The verdict on the synthetic base is partially certified. The coarse existence detector is validated and profile-robust; per-KC resolution, active per-KC rank, pre-fix saturation robustness, neural faithfulness, and second-tier rate reliability are each explicitly not earned, and the two failed legs, the saturation null since repaired and the reliability wiring, are named fixable [G23]. Only the licensed portion crosses to real data.

The synthetic-certified cohort-grain existence gate transfers. It fires on real KDD Algebra under the saturation-aware null, on the unsaturated KC subset (257 of 515 KCs, the other 258 masked as near-mastered under the rule fixed at repair time, before any real-data run), at one seed and one cohort split, with B=99 permutation replicates and p at the 0.01 floor, the statistic beating all replicates [R1]. The fire's arithmetic is a bed statistic of 6113.8 over 1,655 analysis learners of 3,310 students [R1]. Four conditions travel with it. The certificate is conditional on bank fidelity at the measured 0.70-0.80 level [G12]. KDD Algebra is a mastery-managed tutoring system, so difficulty and opportunity are not scheduled independently, an ordering confound we name rather than resolve. Bank-error propagation has been measured for the association sign [S8], not for the growth gate, so the growth-side sensitivity to bank error is unmeasured. And the bed verdict's sensitivity to the near-mastered masking threshold is likewise unmeasured. Per-KC discovery on real KDD remains 0 of 515 [R1].

The fire validates the readout; it does not discover growth. Model-free first-attempt curves on KDD rise 12-19 points over the first ten opportunities in every KC model, despite 82-86% overall correctness [R5]. Growth on this bed was never in question. What the fire shows is that the certified instrument, transferred without retuning, reports that known growth through the same machinery that stayed silent on every null twin and, after repair, on the saturated twin.

The silence is the designed other half of the pair. Random-40k Junyi shows no model-detectable growth, a bed statistic of -11763.8 with 0 of 40 per-KC discoveries, and a deliberately biased first-40k sample lands at a near-identical -11038.9, ruling out a sampling artifact [R2]. Junyi's raw aggregate growth exists in triage, so the silence bounds the method on this bed rather than showing that these learners do not learn. What the pair supports so far is a cross-bed contrast consistent with a data-density account. On mean rows per student, the ladder quantity of Figure 5, the silent bed sits at 105 against firing KDD's roughly 2,688, while its median practice, a different statistic kept off that axis, is about 8 opportunities per student [R2]. The two beds also differ in platform, curriculum, KC scheme, and population, so density is the designed explanation, not yet the demonstrated one. Figure 5 presents the pair on the mean-rows-per-student axis, with the deep-Junyi cell drawn pending and the breadth clause printed on the panel.

One cell would turn the contrast into a measured boundary, and it is pending. A deep-Junyi cohort sits between the two verdicts on the mean-rows-per-student axis (105 for silent Junyi, 557 for the deep cohort, 2,688 for firing KDD), and the run is in flight after an out-of-memory restart [P1]. Either outcome is informative. Detection would confirm the thin-data explanation of the silence; continued silence would narrow the boundary interval toward the deep end. The map carries the cell as pending, drafted for both outcomes.

The growth arm closes partially certified. Coarse twin-level detection is certified on the synthetic harness and confirmed on one deep real bed, with a designed cross-bed contrast consistent with a density boundary and no contradiction between beds; per-KC resolution, ACT magnitude and rank, pre-fix saturation robustness, neural faithfulness, and second-tier reliability remain unearned [R3]. Real-bed reporting is licensed at Tier 1 only, population-grain existence claims [R4]. The higher tiers for per-KC and per-learner rates were never exercised, and the pre-registration already names the honest outcome should the per-learner tier fail at every stratum, existence yes, individual rates unreliable at this density [P9].

## 6. Signed cross-KC association: caught mid-cycle

The growth readout completed one certify-then-confirm cycle (Section 5). The signed cross-KC readout is the protocol's second instantiation, caught mid-cycle, and this section reports the interrupted state as the result. The harness issued one licensed sentence at one named operating point. It measured the dose floor beneath that sentence, measured two conditions that move the floor, and refuted two stronger readings of the same numbers. Every real-data question sits behind a scope wall. No association claim about any real dataset appears in this paper; the real beds touch this arm only through the triage and planned-work material of Section 4.

### 6.1 Feasibility and sign recovery

The arm opened with CT0, the pre-registered power precondition, a make-or-break study that had to establish recoverability before the certification battery ran. The harness plants known cross-KC edges among three KCs (D=3), a facilitation edge at +0.05 and an interference edge at -0.02, doses anchored to the field's population learning rate of 0.1 log-odds per opportunity (Koedinger et al., 2023) [M12], and asks whether the fitted cross-KC matrix recovers their signs. CT0's verdict was inconclusive but alive. No cell of its power grid cleared the full pre-registered bar at D=3, yet the fail-fast kill, sign unidentifiability, did not fire; the best cell reached a sign-classification F1 of 0.889 against a random-sign baseline of 0.254 [S1].

Recovery itself is not the problem. Fitted facilitation coefficients land at +0.046 to +0.060 against the true +0.05, and interference at -0.018 to -0.025 against the true -0.02, at every tested learner count on the single-tag profile (one KC per item, the KDD-shaped density). At N=500 with decoupling 0.90 (the pair-separation measure of Section 4), sign accuracy and negative-half F1 both reach 1.000, seed-consistent [S2]. What failed the bar is discrimination. True-zero edges leak to fitted magnitudes of 0.008 to 0.022, overlapping the 0.02 negative dose, while the positive dose sits 2 to 3 times above the leakage [S3]. The two halves of the readout therefore part ways. The positive half is sign-separable with a measured ladder, F1 of at least 0.889 near 1,700 effective observations per edge and 1.000 by roughly 4,300 and 17,500 [S4]. The negative half has no stable threshold at the 0.02 negative reference dose under the default trainer. Its F1 is 1.000 at N=500 and decoupling 0.90 yet 0.000 at N=1000 and N=2000, with the matched-null band, the 95th percentile of fitted magnitudes on the no-transfer twin (Section 3), drifting between 0.012 and 0.049 as data grow [S5]. The band also grows with training, from 0.0152 at 500 epochs to 0.0192 at 1,500, while held-out NLL moves only from 0.3527 to 0.3520, reproducing in the transfer setting the flat-basin pathology the stationarity study caught in the growth arm [S6] [G22].

### 6.2 The repair that failed, and what that taught

CT0 left behind a repair hypothesis and an explanation, and the follow-up study refuted both. The hypothesis held that a stronger L1 sparsity penalty with earlier stopping would pin the leaking true zeros while sparing the true edges. The winner rule was frozen in code before any result, and tuning ran only on held-out seeds. No configuration qualifies. Across the penalty ladder the true positive edge shrinks from 0.0614 to 0.0237 while the null band grows from 0.0145 to 0.0482, at either epoch ceiling (500 or 1,500); the penalty shrinks real edges faster than zeros [S9] (Figure 7a).

The explanation fared no better. CT0 had read the leak as a trainer artifact that better optimization would remove. Instead, even at the weakest rung of the ladder the band grows with epochs, from 0.0145 to 0.0172, and every stronger rung makes discrimination worse; the true-zero leak is a small-signal identifiability property of the objective at this grain, not a regularization artifact [S10]. A re-derivation of the stopping rule closed the same way, and the untuned default stands for everything that follows [S20].

The failed repair fixed the shape of the claim. If the leak cannot be regularized away, the capability on offer is not a cleaned matrix but a measured detectable dose, with the conditions that move it measured too.

### 6.3 The certified floor and its movers

That measurement is the arm's central positive result. The detectable-dose floor for the negative, interference-side edge is |g| = 0.04, twice the field-anchored negative reference dose of 0.02 [S11].[^floor-tag] The floor is certified on the synthetic harness at one named operating point (D=3, N=500, single-tag density, calibrated bank, untuned default trainer), under pre-registered per-dose bars on clean certification seeds 0 to 4, with tuning confined to held-out seeds 100 to 102, where it produced no winner [M5]. The calibrated-bank condition is idealized. The program's own bank recovers at 0.70 to 0.80 against its 0.9 bar [G12], and the bank-error probe below measures what happens at that fidelity. At the certified dose the fitted edge lands at -0.0425 with a separation of 3.09 above the null band, negative-half F1 of 1.000, positive-half F1 of 0.933, and a false-edge rate of 0.050. Doses 0.01 and 0.02 fail, and 0.08 fails as well, on 2 of 20 true-zero crossings rather than on recovery [S11] (Figure 6). Floor measurement is the certified capability. Floor transport is refused. The number is the first measured point of a power surface, in the two lineages of power-to-detect-change analysis (Hertzog et al., 2006) and minimum-detectable-effect reporting (Card et al., 2020), not a constant of the readout.

Two movers of that surface are measured rather than presumed (Figure 7b). At multi-tag density, the EdNet-shaped profile where items tag 2.2 KCs on average, sign recovery collapses outright. The planted +0.05 edge fits at 0.004 to 0.018, the interference edge at -0.002 to -0.013, and the best sign F1 is 0.444, because co-tagged bystanders make cross-KC transfer collinear with a KC's own gain [S7]. This is a per-density identifiability limit, not a tuning failure. Bank error is the second mover, and it is the measured face of the idealized-bank condition above. With item difficulties perturbed to the bank's measured recovery floor (rank correlation 0.759, the probe point within Section 4's measured 0.70 to 0.80 band), sign F1 falls from 0.889 to 0.756 at N=500, the fitted positive dose inflates to about 0.08, and leakage reaches about 0.028, while a positive-half F1 of 0.78 to 0.83 survives; the sign claim is bank-sensitive [S8]. Both movers enter the licensing map as floor-modifier cells (Table 5).

The binding constraint is neither mover but a dose-independent false-edge background. In every dose row, 1 to 3 of 20 true-zero cells cross the null band, roughly 5 to 15 percent, and the background does not shrink as dose grows [S12]. Any real-data use of per-edge existence therefore requires multiplicity control before a single edge is read. At K=515 KCs there are roughly 265,000 ordered pairs, so this background implies tens of thousands of false signed edges before correction. Per-edge existence at deployment scale is refused pending the pre-registered full-K arms, and the multiplicity control this program certified was certified on per-KC growth statistics, never on the cross-KC matrix [S12] [M10].

### 6.4 Controls, the kill, and the licensed sentence

Three controls fix the grammar of what the floor licenses. The first is the phantom-transfer control, which passes as pre-registered on the synthetic harness. Freeing the per-learner transfer multiplier that Section 4 pins to 1 fabricates transfer on the no-transfer twin in 5 of 5 seeds; the 95th-percentile per-learner transfer reads 0.13 to 0.33 freed against 0.005 to 0.016 pinned, over a null band of 0.0138 [S13]. The control confirms that the tail metric would see such fabrication if the pin were released. The pin itself stays on the structural grounds established in Section 4 [S17] [S18] [S19]; this outcome neither re-earns nor overturns it.

The second is the order-shuffle arm, a negative control in the textbook sense (Lipsitch et al., 2010), and it is the kill that defines the claim. Re-drawing each learner's cross-KC interleaving preserves accumulated dose while destroying temporal order, so a genuinely temporal readout should collapse. It does not. The positive magnitude retains +0.0418 of +0.0564, a ratio of 0.741, and the negative retains -0.0347 of -0.0425, a ratio of 0.815, both far above the pre-registered 0.10 collapse bar [S14] (Figure 7c). The temporal-transfer reading is refuted, and what the model reads is signed dose-association. The lag-driven component of the estimate is bounded by the 25.9 and 18.5 percent shuffle drops, and nothing certified separates those drops from noise [S15].

The third control is owed, and its absence bounds the claim from above. The causal cumulative-dose reading, that practicing a source KC raises or lowers a target KC, is unearned rather than refuted; the shuffle did not touch it. Dose-association coincides with the causal effect only under exogenous practice scheduling, which holds in the harness by construction, reference edges connecting only KC pairs that share no co-tagged item and a scheduler blind to learner state [M15], and which is untested on real beds. The endogenous-scheduler twin that would probe it, together with a real-bed schedule-selection control, is pre-registered and unexecuted [P5].

What survives is one sentence, written here in full. On the synthetic harness (D=3, N=500, single-tag density, calibrated bank, untuned default trainer), the fitted cross-KC matrix licenses per-edge signed dose-association, accumulated source practice associated with raised or lowered target performance [S16]. The sign is seed-consistent and the magnitude near truth at about 4.3 thousand effective observations per edge. The positive half is robust from the +0.05 dose with a separation of about 4. The negative half is detectable from the measured 0.04 floor and inherits its certification caveat [S16].

The sentence is narrow, and its novelty is a conjunction rather than the sign. LTKT models positive and negative concept transfer, but its signs are stipulated inputs, computed by a co-occurrence heuristic before training and validated by prediction alone (Xu et al., 2025). HawkesKT learns signed cross-effects from data, but never certifies them; its one external check is ranking agreement with expert annotators (NDCG 0.83) on a collapsed positive-only score, and its negative direction is conditioned on responses rather than being a skill-to-skill estimate (Wang et al., 2021). A dedicated sweep found no readout that is recovered from the fitted model, certified against ground truth on a synthetic harness with designed confound arms, and accompanied by a measured detectable-dose floor. That conjunction, to our knowledge the first certified signed cross-KC readout, is the claim; the sweep's scope and sources are part of the released evidence registry [S21].

A demoted quantity is still worth certifying, for two reasons in the spirit of Kane's argument-based validation (Kane, 2013), in which every interpretive claim is a chain of separately licensed inferences. First, signed dose-association is what the field's signed readouts estimate at best, whatever their captions say, so certifying it and refusing everything above it is an audit finding about current interpretive practice. Second, it is a screening quantity with a pre-registered upgrade path; the scheduling control and the external corroboration below carry named licensing conditions, and only passing them raises the claim.

The rest of the cycle is stated as owed. The full certification battery has been re-baselined to the measured 0.04 floor at the same operating point and has not been run. Its arms, anti-gaming sign gates with dual baselines, a six-null confound battery, per-learner tail bands, a mismatched-generator arm that tests whether data from a different generating process can pass certification it should fail, a symmetric saturation-side refusal, and full-K scaling at K = 189, 515, and about 835, are pre-registered with frozen bars and the status unexecuted (Table A1) [P3]. External corroboration of the positive half against the junyi15 prerequisite graph is planned and has not run, conditional on that bed clearing the still-unmeasured exercise-grain positivity bar of Section 4 (Appendix D) [P4] [S25]; the decoupling admission bar itself must be re-certified on this harness rather than inherited [P7]. The design also pre-registers a clean-negative verdict, three named triggers under which the learned-signed claim is reported unsupported, so that failure lands as a stated negative rather than a soft partial verdict [P6]. The hard scope wall around real data is an output of the same discipline. Until the battery runs, no real-data association sentence is licensed, and none appears in this paper.

[^floor-tag]: The campaign's records disagree on the strength tag for the dose floor, the false-edge background, and the phantom control (S11, S12, S13). The primary verdict file and the campaign ledger tag all three certified, measured on clean seeds under frozen pre-registered bars; a later summary retells the identical results as synthetic-only. An adjudication upheld the certified tag on the grounds that procedural rigor and data realm are separate axes. This paper adopts the conservative composite, certified on the synthetic harness, in all prose. The released evidence registry carries both records and the adjudication.

Four reporting rules fall out of the map.

- **Saturation-aware null first.** No growth claim on any bed until the bed decision excludes near-mastered KCs; the naive reference false-fires where practice succeeded [G8, R1].
- **Full-pair-population check before any top-K read.** Volume-ranked pairs can be adversarially unrepresentative; the top-30 junyi15 pairs clear decoupling 6.7 percent of the time, the full population 72.2 percent [M9].
- **Multiplicity control before any per-edge existence claim.** The false-edge background is dose-independent, and the certified BH control was earned on per-KC growth statistics under matched dependence, never on the influence matrix [S12, M10].
- **Real-bed tiers.** Reporting is licensed at Tier 1, coarse population claims only; the per-KC and per-learner tiers, never exercised, keep their pre-registered honest-outcome bars [R4, P9].

The legend carries the arithmetic that disciplines the association column. At K=515 there are roughly 265,000 ordered pairs, so a 5 to 15 percent false-edge background implies tens of thousands of false signed edges before correction [S12]. It also names the map's thinnest point, the KDD fire at one seed and one cohort split, the sole real-data positive under a certified headline [R1]. The map's strongest finding lives only in the union of its arms. The growth arm's real-data pair, deep KDD firing while thin Junyi stays silent [R2], and the association arm's synthetic collapse at multi-tag density [S7] are findings of the same kind, a data-regime boundary of a readout mapped by the same protocol, visible side by side only here. The map is not complete, and its incompleteness is the thesis. Claims stop where certification stops, and every pending cell says so in place.

## 8. Discussion

What this paper offers for reuse is the protocol. It ran end to end once, through the growth arm's completed certify-then-confirm cycle, and transferred once, to the association arm caught mid-cycle; that is the generality evidence claimed [M1]. The verdicts do not transfer. Every cell of the licensing map (Table 5) binds a named instrument, bed, and operating point, so a new model, KC scheme, or density regime owes new cells, filled by the same machinery, matched twins, frozen bars, permutation nulls, confound arms, seed discipline [M2]. The association arm's sign recovery collapses at multi-tag density and degrades under measured bank error [S7][S8]; nothing established at one operating point carries to another by assumption. The map travels as a worked example with the harness to re-check it.

The two arms belong in one paper because the map's strongest single finding exists only in their union, the paired data-regime boundaries Section 7 sets side by side [R2][S7]. The protocol claim also needs both. The growth arm shows the gate licensing a claim, repairing a diagnosed failure, and confirming on real data. The association arm shows it refusing, demoting, and stopping, evidence that the gate is not rigged toward either outcome. Separated, each arm supports less. The growth arm alone is a narrow paper stripped of its generality evidence and its map; the association arm alone is a synthetic-only pilot at three KCs with its battery unexecuted. The asymmetry is therefore not filed under limitations. It is stated wherever the pair appears, beginning with the section titles, one completed certify-then-confirm cycle, plus the protocol's second instantiation caught mid-cycle.

Certification caught real defects here, yet its guarantee ends at the failure modes the harness contains. The two-profile bracket supports robustness to density only, not to the generating process, because the twins share the harness's model family [M2]. The saturation inversion is the internal probe of that limit, the harness catching its own reference model converting a ceiling into a confident positive [G5][G6], and the no-growth twin catching the optimizer fabricating growth [G22]. Both episodes are the protocol auditing its own assumptions. Why a quantity as demoted as signed dose-association is still worth certifying was argued in Section 6.4, and the argument rests there.

Two machinery findings are promoted to results because any replication will meet them. Volume-ranked top-K pair selection can be adversarially unrepresentative; junyi15's top-30 prerequisite pairs clear decoupling 6.7 percent of the time while the full pair population clears 72.2 percent, so every top-K read is checked against its full population first [M9]. And real-scale permutation nulls need a memory-bounded deployment mode, bound to the budget the scheduler actually enforces rather than to node RAM; deep-cohort sequence shapes still undercount, and a 200G run died at 64 minutes before the mode existed [E3]. The rest of the engineering ledger sits in Appendix C.

The protocol also caught its own errors, and they are reported as results. Both pre-convergence reads of the active posture were optimization artifacts in opposite directions, a fabricated rise and a spurious silence caught by the no-growth twin; the stationarity-gated trainer that repaired them was accepted at roughly 59 times the old training cost [G22][E4]. A code review of the real-bed bridge found one blocking defect, load statistics computed over a different row universe than the triage they were checked against, fixed with a targeted fixture [E7]; even repaired, the agreement is mostly self-consistency, with only two independent checks [E6]. The second-tier rate-reliability verdict could not be issued because its split-half leg is dead code, a harness wiring gap [G20]. And for about a day and a half, progress was mismeasured as grid fraction while a complete reportable result sat un-summarized [E19].

The relation to the item-side audit this work extends (Anonymous, under review; Section 1) is a boundary, not an overlap. That audit showed static, single-skill item parameters in prediction-trained KT models can be stable and wrong, with a priced decision cost in simulation; its territory ends where this paper's begins, person-side dynamics and multi-KC structure. Nothing here re-litigates those verdicts or assumes their repair carries over, since person-side summaries stayed path-sensitive even where item maps agreed. What is inherited is the posture, that prediction never certifies a readout. The cost of misreading a growth or cross-KC readout is taken from that precedent on argument; no decision-cost experiment ran on either readout.

Each limitation is already delivered as a result; we collect them once. The map holds one real-data positive, earned on the unsaturated 257 of 515 KCs at one seed and one cohort split, making replication breadth its thinnest point [R1]. The saturation repair was verified on the twin set that exposed the failure, and the bed verdict's sensitivity to the near-mastered masking threshold is unmeasured [G8][R1]. Every association certificate is scoped to the synthetic harness at the named operating point of Section 6.3; no real-data association sentence exists in this paper [S11][S16]. The 0.70-0.80 bank-fidelity floor, against its 0.9 bar, rides into every bank-dependent verdict, and bank-error propagation is measured only for the association sign, never for the growth gate [G12][S8]. The Junyi silence bounds the method, not the learners; raw aggregate growth exists there in triage [R2]. junyi15's growth magnitude carries an unresolved within-topic sequencing confound [R6], the Q-matrix circularity guard's 50 percent threshold would miss subtler partial circularity [E12], and decline is out of scope by construction [G21].

What certification would take for the pending cells is already priced, because each is pre-registered. The deep-Junyi cell is in flight; detection would confirm the thin-data explanation of the silence, continued silence would narrow the boundary interval, and either outcome is informative [P1]. PAS-N2 owes a measured faithfulness verdict before any sentence beyond its construction guarantee [P2]. The association arm owes the re-baselined battery at its frozen bars [P3], the endogenous-scheduler control before any causal reading [P5], and external corroboration, itself gated on re-certifying the decoupling bar and measuring Junyi's exercise-grain positivity [P4][P7]. If the battery fails, the pre-registered clean-negative verdict reports the claim unsupported, with no softer fallback [P6]. The real-bed per-KC and per-learner tiers keep their frozen bars and named honest outcomes [P9]. Whichever verdicts arrive, the map absorbs them; claims stop where certification stops.

## 9. Conclusion

The paper's deliverable is the licensing map and the harness that re-checks it, and its contribution is a claim grammar, one completed certify-then-confirm cycle plus the protocol's second instantiation caught mid-cycle. Two readouts are licensed. Growth existence at cohort grain is certified on both synthetic density profiles and confirmed on real KDD Algebra under the saturation-aware null, on the unsaturated 257 of 515 KCs at one seed and one cohort split, with p at the B=99 floor of 0.01 [G1][R1]. The gate's correct silence on thin-practice Junyi gives a cross-bed contrast consistent with a density boundary of the method, its deciding cell pending [R2][P1]. Per-edge signed dose-association is certified on the synthetic harness at the named operating point of Section 6.3, with a measured negative-edge detectable-dose floor of 0.04; the calibrated-bank condition of that operating point is idealized relative to the program's own measured bank [S16][S11][G12]. Around them the protocol refuses, with the mechanism attached, per-KC resolution at both density extremes [G9], active-posture magnitude and rank [G13], the field-default tracker's faithfulness on the synthetic twins [G17], and the temporal reading of the association [S14]. The causal reading and the remaining association battery are pre-registered and unexecuted, and nothing beyond them is promised [P5][P3]. The paper moves no AUC and never claims to. It states, with ground truth rather than opinion, which interpretive sentences these readouts license and exactly where they stop. The protocol transfers; the verdicts do not.
