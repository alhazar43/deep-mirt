# 8. Discussion

What this paper offers for reuse is the protocol. It ran end to end once, through the growth arm's completed certify-then-confirm cycle, and transferred once, to the association arm caught mid-cycle; that is the generality evidence claimed [M1]. The verdicts do not transfer. Every cell of the licensing map (Table 5) binds a named instrument, bed, and operating point, so a new model, KC scheme, or density regime owes new cells, filled by the same machinery, matched twins, frozen bars, permutation nulls, confound arms, seed discipline [M2]. The association arm's sign recovery collapses at multi-tag density and degrades under measured bank error [S7][S8]; nothing established at one operating point carries to another by assumption. The map travels as a worked example, plus the harness to re-check it.

The two arms belong in one paper because the map's strongest single finding exists only in their union, the paired identifiability boundaries Section 7 sets side by side [R2][S7]. The protocol claim also needs both. The growth arm shows the gate can license, repair, and confirm; the association arm shows it can refuse, demote, and stop, the evidence that the gate is not rigged toward either outcome. Split, the parts are worth less. The growth arm alone is a narrow paper stripped of its generality evidence and its map; the association arm alone is a synthetic-only pilot at three skills with its battery unexecuted. The asymmetry is therefore not filed under limitations. It is stated wherever the pair appears, beginning with the section titles, one completed certify-then-confirm cycle, plus the protocol's second instantiation caught mid-cycle.

Certification did real work here, yet it remains necessary, not sufficient. A certificate licenses a claim against the failure modes the harness was built to expose, and nothing under those it does not contain. The two-profile bracket supports robustness to density only, not to the generating process, because the twins share the harness's model family [M2]. The saturation inversion is the internal probe of that limit, the harness catching its own reference model converting a ceiling into a confident positive [G5][G6], and the no-growth twin catching the optimizer fabricating growth [G22]. Both episodes are the protocol auditing its own assumptions. Why a quantity as demoted as signed dose-association is still worth certifying was argued in Section 6.4, and the argument rests there.

Two machinery findings are promoted to results because any replication will meet them. Volume-ranked top-K pair selection can be adversarially unrepresentative; junyi15's top-30 prerequisite pairs clear decoupling 6.7 percent of the time while the full pair population clears 72.2 percent, so every top-K read is checked against its full population first [M9]. And real-scale permutation nulls need a memory-bounded deployment mode, bound to the budget the scheduler actually enforces rather than to node RAM; deep-cohort sequence shapes still undercount, and a 200G run died at 64 minutes before the mode existed [E3]. The rest of the engineering ledger sits in Appendix C.

The protocol also caught its own errors, and they are reported as results. Both pre-convergence reads of the active posture were optimization artifacts in opposite directions, a fabricated rise and a spurious silence caught by the no-growth twin; the stationarity-gated trainer that repaired them was accepted at roughly 59 times the old training cost [G22][E4]. A code review of the real-bed bridge found one blocking defect, load statistics computed over a different row universe than the triage they were checked against, fixed with a targeted fixture [E7]; even repaired, the agreement is mostly self-consistency, with only two independent checks [E6]. The second-tier rate-reliability verdict could not be issued because its split-half leg is dead code, a harness wiring gap [G20]. And for about a day and a half, progress was mismeasured as grid fraction while a complete reportable result sat un-summarized [E19].

The relation to the item-side audit this work extends (Section 1) is a boundary, not an overlap. That audit showed static, single-skill item parameters in prediction-trained KT models can be stable and wrong, with a priced decision cost in simulation; its territory ends where this paper's begins, person-side dynamics and multi-KC structure. Nothing here re-litigates those verdicts or assumes their repair carries over, since person-side summaries stayed path-sensitive even where item maps agreed. What is inherited is the posture, that prediction never certifies a readout. The cost of misreading a growth or cross-skill readout is argued from that precedent, not measured here; no decision-cost experiment ran on either readout.

Each limitation is already delivered as a result; we collect them once. The map holds one real-data positive, earned on the unsaturated 257 of 515 KCs at one seed and one cohort split, making replication breadth its thinnest point [R1]. Every association certificate is scoped to the synthetic harness at D=3, N=500, single-tag density, calibrated bank, untuned default trainer; no real-data association sentence exists in this paper [S11][S16]. The 0.70-0.80 bank-fidelity floor, against its 0.9 bar, rides into every bank-dependent verdict, and bank-error propagation is measured only for the association sign, never for the growth gate [G12][S8]. The Junyi silence bounds the method, not the learners; raw aggregate growth exists there in triage [R2]. junyi15's growth magnitude carries an unresolved within-topic sequencing confound [R6], the Q-matrix circularity guard's 50 percent threshold would miss subtler partial circularity [E12], and decline is out of scope by construction [G21].

What certification would take for the pending cells is already priced, because each is pre-registered. The deep-Junyi cell is in flight; detection would confirm the thin-data explanation of the silence, continued silence would narrow the boundary interval, and either outcome is informative [P1]. PAS-N2 owes a measured faithfulness verdict before any sentence beyond its construction guarantee [P2]. The association arm owes the re-baselined battery at its frozen bars [P3], the endogenous-scheduler control before any causal reading [P5], and external corroboration, itself gated on re-certifying the decoupling bar and measuring Junyi's exercise-grain positivity [P4][P7]. If the battery fails, the pre-registered clean-negative verdict reports the claim unsupported, not softened [P6]. The real-bed per-KC and per-learner tiers keep their frozen bars and named honest outcomes [P9]. Whichever verdicts arrive, the map absorbs them; claims stop where certification stops.
