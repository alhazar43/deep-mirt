# 1. Introduction

Deep knowledge-tracing (KT) models are routinely read as measurement instruments. Their latent states are published as growth curves, per-skill mastery, and cross-skill influence graphs, and the nearest published neighbors, Deep-IRT (Yeung 2019), PSI-KT (Zhou et al. 2024), HawkesKT (Wang et al. 2021), and LTKT (Xu et al. 2025), validate those readouts by prediction accuracy alone, never by testing whether the estimator recovers known structure. A literature sweep found both claims this paper targets, signed influence recovery and a trustworthiness audit of KT readouts, genuinely open [S21].

Prediction cannot certify a readout, and raw learning curves cannot either; they are attrition-biased, since late-practice learners are not a random sample of those who started (Nixon et al. 2018). Two results in Section 5 sharpen the point. The clean raw-statistic separation between growth and no-growth data on a deep-practice profile is partly a density artifact, gone at thin practice [G3], and a gate with a misspecified no-growth reference fires confidently on near-ceiling data, a false fire raw curves cannot diagnose [G5].

A companion paper prices the stakes on the item side. Auditing static, single-skill KT models, it found item-parameter readouts can be stable and wrong. Accuracy, rerun stability, and split-half reliability all pass while the shared readout channel's item parameters err badly against ground truth, with a decision cost in simulation, roughly doubled adaptive-test length and 2.3 percentage points of added cut-score misclassification. Even where item maps agreed, person-side summaries remained path-sensitive, a warning aimed at exactly this territory. This paper takes up what that audit disclaims, person-side dynamics and multi-skill structure; the cost of a misread growth curve or influence graph is argued from that precedent, not measured here.

Our answer is a certification protocol that asks, before any readout is read, whether the estimator recovers known truth [M1]. Its instruments are matched synthetic twins calibrated to named real datasets with the target effect switched on or off by construction, bracketing two density extremes [M2], pre-registered bars and kill conditions frozen before any run [M3], permutation nulls [M6], designed confound arms, and end-to-end seed discipline [M5], with refusals published as first-class results. A readout is certified when it passes those bars on the synthetic harness under seed discipline; certification is necessary, not sufficient. The protocol runs end to end once and transfers once.

This paper makes four contributions.

1. **A certification protocol for the latent-state readouts of multi-skill KT models.** Matched twins, frozen bars and kill conditions, permutation nulls, confound arms, seed discipline, refusals as deliverables [M1, M2, M3, M4, M8]. Every ingredient has owners (Section 2); the claimed novelty is the assembly for this object and its outputs, not the epistemic move.

2. **A certified growth-existence readout with one closed certify-then-confirm loop.** The passive gate certifies at cohort grain on both density-extreme twin profiles, silent on every null twin and at the permutation floor on every growth twin, reporting presence, never size [G1, G2, G4]. Its one diagnosed failure, a false fire on the saturated twin, is traced to reference-model misspecification and repaired with a saturation-aware null [G5, G6, G8]. The repaired instrument fires on real KDD Algebra under the saturation-aware null, on the unsaturated skill subset (257 of 515), at breadth one seed and one cohort split (B=99, p=0.01), and stays correctly silent on thin-practice Junyi, mapping a data-density boundary of the method rather than an absence of learning, one boundary cell still pending [R1, R2, P1]. Model-free curves already rise on KDD, so the fire validates the instrument rather than discovering growth [R5]; the arm rolls up as partially certified [R3].

3. **The first certified signed cross-skill readout, honestly demoted to dose-association at a named operating point.** Signs are recovered from the fitted model near truth [S2], and on the synthetic harness at D=3 skills, N=500 learners, single-tag density, calibrated bank, untuned default trainer, the readout earns exactly one licensed sentence, per-edge signed dose-association with a measured negative-edge detectable-dose floor of 0.04, twice the field-anchored reference dose [S16, S11]. The paper shows that floor move under density and bank error [S7, S8], and a measured false-edge background mandates multiplicity control on any real-data use [S12]. A phantom-transfer control passes [S13], an order-shuffle control refutes the temporal reading [S14], the causal reading is left unearned pending a pre-registered scheduling control [P5], and the remaining battery is pre-registered and unexecuted [P3].

4. **The licensing map, with refusals as first-class, mechanism-attached results.** Per-skill growth resolution fails at both density extremes with the same failure signature [G9], growth magnitude and per-skill ranking fail their corridors [G13], the field-representative shared-state neural tracker fails the faithfulness battery at production scale [G17], the association sign collapses at multi-tag density [S7], and decline is out of scope by construction [G21]. Every audited cell is labeled certified on the synthetic harness, confirmed on real data, refused with mechanism, or pending pre-registered; the deliverable is the map plus the harness to re-check it (Table 5).

The architecture is asymmetric by design, one completed certify-then-confirm cycle (growth) plus the protocol's second instantiation caught mid-cycle (signed association), reported as far as it got. The certificates attach to the paper's own instruments, the passive gate and a deliberately constrained transfer readout (Section 4); the field-default tracker we test is refused, a result Section 5 delivers [G17]. The protocol transfers; the verdicts do not. Sections 2 through 4 set out lineage, protocol, and instruments; Sections 5 and 6 execute the two arms; Section 7 assembles the map; Sections 8 and 9 weigh what transfers and what it costs. The paper moves no AUC and never claims to. It states, with ground truth rather than opinion, which interpretive sentences these readouts license and exactly where they stop.
