# 7. The licensing map

Table 5 assembles every verdict into the paper's deliverable. Rows are claim tiers, from pooled growth existence through per-KC resolution, magnitude and rank, and per-learner rates and reliability, to signed cross-skill association, its temporal and causal readings, and decline. Columns are data regimes, the KDD-shaped and EdNet-shaped synthetic profiles, the real deep and thin beds, and multi-tag density. Each cell carries one of four states, certified on the synthetic harness, confirmed on real data, refused with mechanism, or pending pre-registered, and prints its map ID. The legend has two layers because the states live in different epistemic realms. Harness certification means the estimator recovered known truth under frozen bars; real-data confirmation means the certified instrument behaved as licensed on a bed whose truth is not known. The association column sits entirely in the harness layer.

The certified layer holds the pooled existence gate on both synthetic density profiles [G1], its null clean on both [G2], and four association cells certified on the synthetic harness at D=3, N=500, single-tag density, calibrated bank, untuned default trainer. These are the negative-edge detectable-dose floor of 0.04 [S11], the false-edge background that mandates multiplicity control [S12], the passing phantom-transfer control [S13], and the licensed sentence itself, per-edge signed dose-association [S16]. The confirmed layer holds exactly two cells. The gate fires on real KDD Algebra under the saturation-aware null, on the unsaturated 257 of 515 KCs, at one seed and one cohort split, B=99, p=0.01 [R1], and stays correctly silent on thin-practice Junyi, mapping a density boundary rather than an absence of learning [R2].

The refused cells outnumber both layers, each carrying its mechanism rather than a bare verdict. Two refusals discipline the headline positives. The raw-statistic separation is partly a density artifact [G3], and the pre-fix gate false-fires on saturation through reference-model misspecification [G5]. The finer-grain tiers refuse across the board. Per-KC resolution fails at both density extremes with the same signature, invariant under a full density inversion [G9], with zero corrected power on the positive control [G10] and rate recovery far below its bar [G11]; the 0.70 to 0.80 bank-fidelity floor against the 0.9 bar rides into every bank-dependent cell [G12]. The active posture is refused as a magnitude estimator and per-KC ranker [G13], no variant stays clean on both nulls and both profiles [G15], and the misfit clause is unusable by opposite numeric routes [G16]. The field-representative shared-state tracker fails the faithfulness battery on both beds at production scale [G17]. Decline is out of scope by construction, a recorded limit [G21]. On the association side, sign recovery collapses at multi-tag density [S7], the sparsity repair is refuted [S9], the trainer-artifact account of the leak is overturned [S10], and the temporal reading is refuted by the order-shuffle control [S14].

Pending cells are visually distinct, never blended into verdicts. The deep-Junyi cell either confirms the thin-data explanation or narrows the boundary interval; both outcomes are informative [P1]. PAS-N2 has no measured faithfulness verdict in any cell [P2]. The re-baselined association battery is pre-registered and unexecuted [P3], external graph corroboration is gated by an unmeasured positivity bar [P4], the causal reading waits on the endogenous-scheduler control [P5], and the finer real-bed reliability tiers have never been exercised [P9].

Four reporting rules fall out of the map.

- **Saturation-aware null first.** No growth claim on any bed until the bed decision excludes near-mastered KCs; the naive reference false-fires where practice succeeded [G8, R1].
- **Full-pair-population check before any top-K read.** Volume-ranked pairs can be adversarially unrepresentative; the top-30 junyi15 pairs clear decoupling 6.7 percent of the time, the full population 72.2 percent [M9].
- **Multiplicity control before any per-edge existence claim.** The false-edge background is dose-independent, and the certified BH control was earned on per-KC growth statistics under matched dependence, never on the influence matrix [S12, M10].
- **Real-bed tiers.** Reporting is licensed at Tier 1, coarse population claims only; the per-KC and per-learner tiers, never exercised, keep their pre-registered honest-outcome bars [R4, P9].

The legend carries the arithmetic that keeps the association column honest. At K=515 there are roughly 265,000 ordered pairs, so a 5 to 15 percent false-edge background implies tens of thousands of false signed edges before correction [S12]. It also names the map's thinnest point, the KDD fire at one seed and one cohort split, the sole real-data positive under a certified headline [R1]. The map's strongest finding lives only in the union of its arms. The growth arm's real-data density boundary, where deep KDD fires and thin Junyi stays silent [R2], and the association arm's synthetic collapse at multi-tag density [S7] are the same kind of finding, an identifiability boundary of a readout mapped by the same protocol, visible side by side only here. The map is not complete, and its incompleteness is the thesis. Claims stop where certification stops, and every pending cell says so in place.
