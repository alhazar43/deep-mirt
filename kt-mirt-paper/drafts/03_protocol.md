# 3. The certification protocol

The protocol enforces one discipline, certify before claim. No latent-state readout is interpreted until the same estimator has recovered known truth on synthetic data, and the real-data phase of each arm begins only once a synthetic certificate licenses it [M1]. Throughout this paper the word certified has an operational meaning. A readout is certified when it passes pre-registered bars on the synthetic harness under seed discipline, with every threshold frozen before any run. Certification is necessary, not sufficient. It licenses a claim against the failure modes the harness was built to expose, and it guarantees nothing under failure modes the harness does not contain. Where the campaign's own records disagreed on the strength of a verdict, the weaker reading governs every sentence here. The protocol's ingredients are individually established practice, and we say so before a reviewer must. Validating inference machinery on data simulated from a known model is the logic of simulation-based calibration (Talts et al., 2018). Randomization tests that a readout must pass before it is trusted are the sanity-check posture of interpretability auditing (Adebayo et al., 2018). Negative controls are standard in epidemiology and causal inference (Lipsitch et al., 2010). What we claim is the assembly of these instruments around the latent-state readouts of knowledge-tracing models, and the output states that assembly produces. Figure 1 sketches the pipeline.

The device that makes the assembly work is the matched twin, a synthetic replica calibrated to the interface statistics of a named real dataset, with growth or cross-skill transfer switched on or off by construction. A detector can then be run against datasets that differ only in whether the effect it reports actually exists. Two profiles at opposite density extremes bracket the range the real beds occupy, a few-deep profile and a many-thin profile, and a result holding at both ends is unlikely to be a quirk of one data shape, so no third profile was needed [M2]. We call the profiles KDD-shaped and EdNet-shaped. The names record calibration targets and never stand in for real-data results. The bracket supports robustness to density only. It says nothing about robustness to the generating process, since the twins share the harness's model family, a limit Section 5 probes from inside and the discussion returns to.

Both designs were pre-registered in full before any run. The growth design's registration (A4, v1.1) froze its estimands, generator, ten-arm battery, and thirteen certification gates ordered positive-control first, together with kill conditions K1 through K7; thresholds may tighten but never loosen, and revisions were capped at two rounds [M3]. The transfer design's registration (A1, v1.1) inherited the two-revision rule and added a power precondition, designed confound twins, a saturation-matched null, kill conditions K-T1 through K-T6, and a pre-registered clean-negative verdict [M4]. Every kill condition carries a named honest verdict, so a dead end is documented rather than buried [M8]. Table 2 lists the gates and kills compactly with their frozen bars and executed verdicts; Appendix A holds the full registry, including the arms that remain pre-registered and unexecuted.

The statistical core is a permutation null built against fitted no-growth references, never against bare zero. A fitted quantity carries a per-seed offset, so observed effects are compared with the 95th percentile of the same quantity fitted on no-effect twins, the matched-null band [M6]. Because the null is a fitted reference rather than a distribution-free construction, the test is size-valid only for the family of no-effect processes that reference spans. Certification is therefore relative to the reference model, a dependence the saturated twin later makes concrete (Section 5). Seed discipline runs end to end. Hyperparameter tuning used held-out seeds 100 through 102 and produced no winner, so the default trainer was never tuned and certification seeds 0 through 4 stayed clean [M5]. Confirmatory claims must be sign-consistent in every seed and significant when seeds are pooled; neural arms run three model seeds as a stated pre-registered compute concession [M7]. Multiplicity control is itself certified rather than assumed. The realized false-discovery rate of the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure is certified on the null twin at both densities, with the dependence-robust Benjamini-Yekutieli variant reported beside every discovery list, and that certificate is scoped to per-skill growth statistics only [M10].

Synthetic effect sizes are anchored to the field rather than chosen for convenience. Growth magnitudes and the reference transfer dose are set from the published population learning rate of 0.1 log-odds per opportunity (Koedinger et al., 2023), per-skill rates are spread twenty-fold so that rank recovery is a meaningful target, and a dose-response sweep at 0.5, 1, 2, and 4 times the reference dose is pre-registered [M12]. The rate estimand is affine-invariant to any positive rescaling of the ability scale and so cannot be biased by scale gauge; displacements and asymptotes are gauge-dependent and are reported only on the frozen anchored scale [M11]. At the existence tier the growth design has exactly three independent inputs, and firing of the actively trained posture alone is never claimed as growth [M14]. Reference transfer edges are placed only between skill pairs sharing no co-tagged item, so synthetic sign recovery measures practice-gated transfer with temporal lag rather than co-observation; same-slot co-observation is tested as a separate confound arm that must not be read as transfer [M15].

The certified object is named exactly. It is the existence of monotone ability gain at cohort grain. Decline is out of scope by construction; the active posture's no-decay pin structurally bars it from representing an individual learner's decline, and decline-side slices are read by passive machinery only [G21]. This recorded scope limit is the protocol's first refused row, entered before any experiment is reported, and it travels into the licensing map like every other verdict.

Finally, the protocol defines refusal as an output class rather than a caveat. Every audited readout cell ends in one of four states. Certified on the synthetic harness means the pre-registered bars passed under seed discipline. Real-data confirmed means a synthetically certified instrument then ran on a real bed and behaved as certified. Refused with mechanism means a bar failed and the failure was traced to a cause that is published with the verdict. Pending pre-registered means the arm is frozen but unexecuted. Sections 5 and 6 deliver verdicts in all four states, and Section 7 assembles them into the licensing map.
