# 5. The growth-existence readout: a completed cycle

This section walks the growth-existence readout through the full cycle the protocol prescribes, certification on matched twins, one diagnosed false fire and its repair, and a real-data confirmation paired with a designed silence. The refusals at finer grain are not caveats to that arc. They are results of the same rank, delivered here with their mechanisms and their lineage.

## 5.1 Certification at cohort grain

The passive existence gate asks one question of a bed, whether pooled ability gain is present at cohort grain, and answers it against a permutation null built from fitted no-growth references. Certification requires silence on the no-growth twin and detection on both growth twins, on both density profiles, under the frozen bars of Section 3.

The gate certifies on both counts. On the no-growth twin the permutation p-values stay dispersed and unremarkable, 0.128 to 0.973 on the KDD-shaped profile and 0.222 to 0.876 on the EdNet-shaped profile, while both growth twins are detected at the permutation floor of p = 0.001 in every seed on both profiles, with no overlap between the two distributions (one EdNet growth cell, ns seed 1, is absent from the artifact set) [G1]. The verdict survives the full density inversion, holding unchanged when the profile flips from few-deep to many-thin. The null is clean at finer grain as well. Per-KC discovery counts under Benjamini-Hochberg (BH) control are zero in all five seeds on the thin profile, whose null twin is in fact tighter than the deep one, with split-half gaps of 0.042 to 0.051 against the KDD-shaped 0.066 to 0.098 [G2]. Figure 2 shows the certification matrix, four twins by five seeds by two profiles.

One result disciplines this headline. The visually clean separation of raw bed statistics on the deep profile, no-growth values at or below 915 against growth values at or above 2375, is partly a density artifact and is not sold as general. At thin density the raw bands overlap, four of five kg growth-twin statistics fall inside the no-growth range, and only the calibrated permutation p-value recovers the separation [G3]. The gate's portability across densities lives in the calibration, not in the raw statistic; the inset of Figure 2 shows the overlap.

A second cap follows from the permutation arithmetic. With 999 replicates the smallest attainable p-value is 1/(999+1) = 0.001, so the gate reports that growth is present and nothing about how much, and it cannot rank one detection above another once both sit at the floor [G4].

## 5.2 The saturation false fire, diagnosed and repaired

The gate's one diagnosed failure is the saturated twin, and it is the most instructive cell in the matrix. That twin carries a true ability rise byte-identical to the kg growth twin's, placed near ceiling where observed performance has little room left to move; the gate must stay silent there. Instead it inverts, firing at the permutation floor in every seed on both profiles, hardest exactly where silence is required [G5]. We read the inversion as the harness doing its intended work, probing the reference model's specification rather than the learners' dynamics.

The mechanism is misspecification near ceiling. The no-growth reference approximates a saturating curve worse than the growth model does, which hands the growth side a near-universal held-out likelihood edge regardless of the true dynamics [G6]. Two independent signals corroborate the diagnosis, a numerical degeneracy that pins the fitted rate uncertainty at its ridge floor, and a split-half reliability gap of 0.259 to 0.292 against a 0.10 tolerance that appears on the saturated twin only [G6]. Ceiling effects are classical measurement territory (Beck & Gong, 2013); what the harness adds is the demonstration, against known truth, that a permutation gate with a misspecified reference converts a ceiling into a confident positive. Density modulates the severity of the false fire but not its existence, a bed-statistic inflation of 3.7x on the deep profile against 1.32x on the thin one [G7].

The repair follows the diagnosis rather than the symptom. A saturation-aware bed null excludes near-mastered KCs from the bed-level decision and touches nothing else; every per-KC statistic is byte-identical before and after. It eliminates the false fire, moving the saturated twin from p = 0.01 to 1.00, while detection on both growth twins holds at p = 0.01 and the no-growth null stays clean [G8]. Figure 3 shows the inversion and the repaired matrix side by side. Every certification statement from here on is relative to the repaired reference, and the repair itself becomes a licensing rule, no growth claim on saturation-prone data without a saturation-aware null.

## 5.3 Refusals at finer grain

Below cohort grain the protocol mostly refuses, and the refusals carry some of the section's most consequential content.

Per-KC resolution leads. The gate that certifies cleanly at cohort grain licenses nothing one level down. Across all five seeds the per-KC discovery count under BH control is 0 of 515 on the deep profile and 0 of 189 on the thin one, by comparable margins, with the same failure signature, invariant under the full density inversion [G9]. The failure is not marginal. On the positive-control growth twin the minimum per-KC p-value is 0.005 against a BH threshold of 9.7e-5, so realized power at this grain is identically zero on both profiles; the second growth twin's discoveries are erratic on the deep profile (65, 0, 55, 0, 0 across seeds), absent on the thin one, and vanish everywhere under the dependence-robust Benjamini-Yekutieli variant [G10]. The pre-registered rate-recovery kill condition supplies the quantitative floor, a rank correlation of about 0.08 between fitted and true per-KC rates against a 0.6 bar at thin density [G11]. The same signature at both density extremes, at comparable margins, is what moves this from a sparsity complaint to an estimator-level verdict, and we infer, labeling it as inference, that more data at these densities will not license per-KC growth claims. The content of the refusal has ancestors. Per-skill non-identifiability has been on record since Beck & Chang (2007), and Khajah et al. (2016) showed that a deep tracker's advantage need not come from per-skill tracking. The deliverable here is the certified, harness-specific verdict with measured margins, not the rediscovery. Figure 4 collects the evidence.

A standing condition rides along with every readout that touches the bank. The frozen difficulty bank recovers at rank correlation 0.70-0.80 against its 0.90 bar, and this bank-fidelity floor travels into each downstream verdict, the growth gate included [G12].

The active posture (ACT) fares worse than the passive gate. It is refused as a magnitude estimator and as a per-KC ranker, undershooting true magnitude by roughly 5-10x, a magnitude ratio of 0.086 against the pre-registered 0.5-1.5 corridor, with per-KC rank correlations of 0.27-0.38 on the deep profile against a 0.5 bar [G13]. The refusal is not total. ACT does recover the presence and direction of growth on both profiles, and per-KC rank is markedly better at thin density, where one variant (act_p1) clears the relaxed positive-control bar at 0.599 [G14]. That discipline does not transfer. No ACT variant is clean on both the null and the growth twins across both profiles simultaneously; at thin density the null breaches on both variants, a 95th-percentile firing level of 0.040 against a 0.02 bar on one and a population level of 0.028 on the other, while the same variant is fully silent on the deep profile [G15]. The misfit clause intended to catch model breakdown is unusable on both profiles by opposite numeric routes, firing on 6-7% of growers where 80% is required on the deep profile and over-firing to non-informativeness on the thin one, where it flags 64-74% of growers but also 58-71% of the silent subset [G16]. Table 3 lays out the corridor, rank, and null-silence grid across variants and profiles.

Reliability closes the ladder. Per-learner split-half reliability, the agreement between gate statistics computed on the two halves of each learner's sequence, passes on the three non-saturated twins on both profiles, with deep-profile gaps of 0.066-0.098 inside the 0.10 tolerance, and is blown out only on the saturated twin, where the 0.259-0.292 gap doubles as a saturation diagnostic [G19]. One tier could not be tested at all. Per-KC rate reliability at the second reporting tier cannot be certified from any existing artifact because the KC-level split-half leg is dead code, defined in the battery but never called by the runner. We report that as a harness wiring gap, an engineering fact rather than a statistical null [G20].

## 5.4 The field-default tracker fails the battery

The certificates above attach to the paper's own instruments. The architecture the field reads by default was put through the same battery and refused. PAS-N1, the field-representative shared-state neural tracker of Section 4, fails the faithfulness battery consistently on both profiles at production scale, across all four audits, the frozen-encoder null, the drill-contamination probe, the order-invariance check, and the direction and reconstruction audit. On the deep profile the first three audits pass 0 of 12 cells each and the reconstruction audit fails 11 of 12, with violation magnitudes of 0.21-0.47; the thin profile repeats the pattern at 0 of 3 per twin, with only partial order-invariance passes [G17]. The scope is the synthetic twins, and we state it as such. What the battery shows is that the field-default architecture does not survive the checks this paper's own instruments are required to pass. One borderline cell is reported honestly, a single reconstruction pass at 0.028 against the 0.10 tolerance on one saturated-twin seed, a genuine exception inside an otherwise-uniform sweep that changes no twin-level verdict [G18].

The decoupled variant PAS-N2 earns no symmetrical sentence. Its audit superiority is a construction guarantee, not a measured result. No faithfulness-battery verdict exists for it in any cell on either profile, and the only number attached to it is a same-ballpark held-out likelihood probe, 0.4326 against PAS-N1's 0.4446 [P2]. The paper claims nothing for it beyond the construction. Table 4 gives the cell-by-cell battery.

## 5.5 Real data: the fire, the silence, the boundary

The synthetic roll-up licenses the real-data step and bounds it. The verdict on the synthetic base is partially certified. The coarse existence detector is validated and profile-robust; per-KC resolution, active per-KC rank, pre-fix saturation robustness, neural faithfulness, and second-tier rate reliability are each explicitly not earned, and the two failed legs, the saturation null since repaired and the reliability wiring, are named fixable [G23]. Only the licensed portion crosses to real data.

The synthetic-certified cohort-grain existence gate transfers. It fires on real KDD Algebra under the saturation-aware null, on the unsaturated KC subset (257 of 515 KCs, the other 258 masked as near-mastered), at breadth one seed and one cohort split (B=99 permutation replicates, p=0.01), and stays correctly silent on thin-practice Junyi, mapping a data-density boundary of the method rather than an absence of learning [R1][R2]. The fire's arithmetic is a bed statistic of 6113.8 beating all 99 replicates, computed over 1,655 analysis learners of 3,310 students [R1]. Three conditions travel with it. The certificate is conditional on bank fidelity at the measured 0.70-0.80 level [G12]. KDD Algebra is a mastery-managed tutoring system, so difficulty and opportunity are not scheduled independently, an ordering confound we name rather than resolve. And bank-error propagation has been measured for the association sign [S8], not for the growth gate, so the growth-side sensitivity to bank error is unmeasured. Per-KC discovery on real KDD remains 0 of 515 [R1].

The fire validates the readout; it does not discover growth. Model-free first-attempt curves on KDD rise 12-19 points over the first ten opportunities in every KC model, despite 82-86% overall correctness [R5]. Growth on this bed was never in question. What the fire shows is that the certified instrument, transferred without retuning, reports that known growth through the same machinery that stayed silent on every null twin and, after repair, on the saturated twin.

The silence is the designed other half of the pair. Random-40k Junyi shows no model-detectable growth, a bed statistic of -11763.8 with 0 of 40 per-skill discoveries, and a deliberately biased first-40k sample lands at a near-identical -11038.9, ruling out a sampling artifact [R2]. Junyi's raw aggregate growth exists in triage, so the verdict is a boundary of the method, not a claim that these learners do not learn; the bed sits at a median of about 8 opportunities per student against KDD's roughly 2,688 interactions per student [R2]. Figure 5 presents the pair on a rows-per-student axis, with the deep-Junyi cell drawn pending and the breadth clause printed on the panel.

One cell of the boundary is pending. A deep-Junyi cohort sits between the two verdicts on the rows-per-student axis (105 for silent Junyi, 557 for the deep cohort, 2,688 for firing KDD), and the run is in flight after an out-of-memory restart [P1]. Either outcome is informative. Detection would confirm the thin-data explanation of the silence; continued silence would narrow the boundary interval toward the deep end. The map carries the cell as pending, drafted for both outcomes.

The growth arm closes partially certified. Coarse twin-level detection is certified on the synthetic harness and confirmed on one deep real bed, with a mapped density boundary and no contradiction between beds; per-KC resolution, ACT magnitude and rank, pre-fix saturation robustness, neural faithfulness, and second-tier reliability remain unearned [R3]. Real-bed reporting is licensed at Tier 1 only, population-grain existence claims [R4]. The higher tiers for per-KC and per-learner rates were never exercised, and the pre-registration already names the honest outcome should the per-learner tier fail at every stratum, existence yes, individual rates unreliable at this density [P9].
