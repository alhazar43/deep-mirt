## 6. Signed cross-skill association: caught mid-cycle

The growth readout completed one certify-then-confirm cycle (Section 5). The signed cross-skill readout is the protocol's second instantiation, caught mid-cycle, and this section reports the interrupted state as the result. The harness issued one licensed sentence at one named operating point, measured the dose floor beneath that sentence and two conditions that move it, and refuted two stronger readings of the same numbers. Every real-data question sits behind a scope wall. No association claim about any real dataset appears in this paper; the real beds touch this arm only through the triage and planned-work material of Section 4.

### 6.1 Feasibility and sign recovery

The arm opened with CT0, the pre-registered power precondition, a make-or-break study that had to establish recoverability before the certification battery was worth its compute. The harness plants known cross-skill edges among three skills (D=3), a facilitation edge at +0.05 and an interference edge at -0.02, doses anchored to the field's population learning rate of 0.1 log-odds per opportunity (Koedinger et al., 2023) [M12], and asks whether the fitted cross-skill matrix recovers their signs. CT0's verdict was inconclusive but alive. No cell of its power grid cleared the full pre-registered bar at D=3, yet the fail-fast kill, sign unidentifiability, did not fire; the best cell reached a sign-classification F1 of 0.889 against a random-sign baseline of 0.254 [S1].

Recovery itself is not the problem. Fitted facilitation coefficients land at +0.046 to +0.060 against the true +0.05 and interference at -0.018 to -0.025 against the true -0.02 at every tested learner count on the single-tag profile (one skill per item, the KDD-shaped density), and at N=500 with decoupling 0.90 (the pair-separation measure of Section 4) sign accuracy and negative-half F1 both reach 1.000, seed-consistent [S2]. What failed the bar is discrimination. True-zero edges leak to fitted magnitudes of 0.008 to 0.022, overlapping the 0.02 negative dose, while the positive dose sits 2 to 3 times above the leakage [S3]. The two halves of the readout therefore part ways. The positive half is sign-separable with a measured ladder, F1 of at least 0.889 near 1,700 effective observations per edge and 1.000 by roughly 4,300 and 17,500 [S4]. The negative half has no stable threshold at the reference dose under the default trainer; its F1 is 1.000 at N=500 and decoupling 0.90 yet 0.000 at N=1000 and N=2000, with the matched-null band, the 95th percentile of fitted magnitudes on the no-transfer twin (Section 3), drifting between 0.012 and 0.049 as data grow [S5]. The band also grows with training, from 0.0152 at 500 epochs to 0.0192 at 1,500 while held-out NLL moves only from 0.3527 to 0.3520, reproducing in the transfer setting the flat-basin pathology the stationarity study caught in the growth arm [S6] [G22].

### 6.2 The repair that failed, and what that taught

CT0 left behind a repair hypothesis and an explanation, and the follow-up study refuted both. The hypothesis held that a stronger L1 sparsity penalty with earlier stopping would pin the leaking true zeros while sparing the true edges. The winner rule was frozen in code before any result, and tuning ran only on held-out seeds. No configuration qualifies. Across the penalty ladder the true positive edge shrinks from 0.0614 to 0.0237 while the null band grows from 0.0145 to 0.0482, at either epoch ceiling (500 or 1,500); the penalty shrinks real edges faster than zeros [S9] (Figure 7a).

The explanation fared no better. CT0 had read the leak as a trainer artifact that better optimization would remove. Instead, even at the weakest rung of the ladder the band grows with epochs, from 0.0145 to 0.0172, and every stronger rung makes discrimination worse; the true-zero leak is a small-signal identifiability property of the objective at this grain, not a regularization artifact [S10]. A re-derivation of the stopping rule closed the same way, and the untuned default stands for everything that follows [S20].

The failed repair fixed the shape of the honest claim. If the leak cannot be regularized away, the capability on offer is not a cleaned matrix but a measured detectable dose, with the conditions that move it measured too.

### 6.3 The certified floor and its movers

That measurement is the arm's central positive result. Under pre-registered per-dose bars on clean certification seeds 0 to 4, with tuning confined to held-out seeds 100 to 102, where it produced no winner [M5], the detectable-dose floor for the negative, interference-side edge is |g| = 0.04, twice the field-anchored reference dose, certified on the synthetic harness at D=3, N=500, single-tag density, calibrated bank, untuned default trainer [S11].[^floor-tag] At that dose the fitted edge lands at -0.0425 with a separation of 3.09 above the null band, negative-half F1 of 1.000, positive-half F1 of 0.933, and a false-edge rate of 0.050; doses 0.01 and 0.02 fail, and 0.08 fails as well, on 2 of 20 true-zero crossings rather than on recovery [S11] (Figure 6). Floor measurement is the certified capability. Floor transport is refused. The number is the first measured point of a power surface, in the two lineages of power-to-detect-change analysis (Hertzog et al., 2006) and minimum-detectable-effect reporting (Card et al., 2020), not a constant of the readout.

Two movers of that surface are measured rather than presumed (Figure 7c). At multi-tag density, the EdNet-shaped profile where items tag 2.2 skills on average, sign recovery collapses outright; the planted +0.05 edge fits at 0.004 to 0.018, the interference edge at -0.002 to -0.013, and the best sign F1 is 0.444, because co-tagged bystanders make cross-skill transfer collinear with a skill's own gain [S7]. This is a per-density identifiability limit, not a tuning failure. Bank error is the second mover. With item difficulties perturbed to the bank's measured recovery floor (rank correlation about 0.759, Section 4), sign F1 falls from 0.889 to 0.756 at N=500, the fitted positive dose inflates to about 0.08, and leakage reaches about 0.028, while a positive-half F1 of 0.78 to 0.83 survives; the sign claim is bank-sensitive [S8]. Both movers enter the licensing map as floor-modifier cells (Table 5).

The binding constraint is neither mover but a dose-independent false-edge background. In every dose row, 1 to 3 of 20 true-zero cells cross the null band, roughly 5 to 15 percent, and the background does not shrink as dose grows [S12]. Any real-data use of per-edge existence therefore requires multiplicity control before a single edge is read. The deployment arithmetic belongs in the open. At K=515 skills there are roughly 265,000 ordered pairs, so this background implies tens of thousands of false signed edges before correction; per-edge existence at deployment scale is refused pending the pre-registered full-K arms, and the multiplicity control this program certified was certified on per-KC growth statistics, never on the cross-skill matrix [S12] [M10].

### 6.4 Controls, the kill, and the licensed sentence

Three controls fix the grammar of what the floor licenses. The first is the phantom-transfer control, which passes as pre-registered on the synthetic harness. Freeing the per-learner transfer multiplier that Section 4 pins to 1 fabricates transfer on the no-transfer twin in 5 of 5 seeds; the 95th-percentile per-learner transfer reads 0.13 to 0.33 freed against 0.005 to 0.016 pinned, over a null band of 0.0138 [S13]. The control confirms that the tail metric would see such fabrication if the pin were released. The pin itself stays on the structural grounds established in Section 4 [S17] [S18] [S19]; this outcome neither re-earns nor overturns it.

The second is the order-shuffle arm, a negative control in the textbook sense (Lipsitch et al., 2010), and it is the kill that defines the claim. Re-drawing each learner's cross-skill interleaving preserves accumulated dose while destroying temporal order, so a genuinely temporal readout should collapse. It does not. The positive magnitude retains +0.0418 of +0.0564, a ratio of 0.741, and the negative retains -0.0347 of -0.0425, a ratio of 0.815, both far above the pre-registered 0.10 collapse bar [S14] (Figure 7b). This is a designed falsification that worked. The temporal-transfer reading is refuted, and what the model reads is signed dose-association. The lag-driven component of the estimate is bounded by the 25.9 and 18.5 percent shuffle drops, and nothing certified separates those drops from noise [S15].

The third control is owed, and its absence bounds the claim from above. The causal cumulative-dose reading, that practicing a source skill raises or lowers a target skill, is unearned rather than refuted; the shuffle did not touch it. Dose-association coincides with the causal effect only under exogenous practice scheduling, which holds in the harness by construction, reference edges connecting only skill pairs that share no co-tagged item and a scheduler blind to learner state [M15], and which is untested on real beds. The endogenous-scheduler twin that would probe it, together with a real-bed schedule-selection control, is pre-registered and unexecuted [P5].

What survives is one sentence, written here in full. On the synthetic harness at D=3, N=500, single-tag density, calibrated bank, untuned default trainer, the fitted cross-skill matrix licenses per-edge signed dose-association, accumulated source practice associated with raised or lowered target performance, sign seed-consistent and magnitude near truth at about 4.3 thousand effective observations per edge, the positive half robust from the +0.05 dose with a separation of about 4, and the negative half detectable from the measured 0.04 floor and inheriting its certification caveat [S16].

The sentence is narrow, and its novelty is a conjunction rather than the sign. LTKT models positive and negative concept transfer, but its signs are stipulated inputs, computed by a co-occurrence heuristic before training and validated by prediction alone (Xu et al., 2025). HawkesKT learns signed cross-effects from data, but never certifies them; its one external check is ranking agreement with expert annotators (NDCG 0.83) on a collapsed positive-only score, and its negative direction is conditioned on responses rather than being a skill-to-skill estimate (Wang et al., 2021). A dedicated sweep found no readout that is recovered from the fitted model, certified against ground truth on a synthetic harness with designed confound arms, and accompanied by a measured detectable-dose floor; that conjunction, the first certified signed cross-skill readout, is the claim [S21].

A demoted quantity is still worth certifying, for two reasons in the spirit of Kane's argument-based validation, in which every interpretive claim is a chain of separately licensed inferences. First, signed dose-association is what the field's signed readouts estimate at best, whatever their captions say, so certifying it and refusing everything above it is an audit finding about current interpretive practice. Second, it is a screening quantity with a pre-registered upgrade path; the scheduling control and the external corroboration below carry named licensing conditions, and only passing them raises the claim.

The rest of the cycle is stated as owed. The full certification battery has been re-baselined to the measured 0.04 floor at the same operating point (D=3, N=500, single-tag density, calibrated bank, untuned default trainer) and has not been run; its arms, anti-gaming sign gates with dual baselines, a six-null confound battery, per-learner tail bands, a mismatched-generator laundering arm, a symmetric saturation-side refusal, and full-K scaling at K = 189, 515, and about 835, are pre-registered with frozen bars and the status unexecuted (Table A1) [P3]. External corroboration of the positive half against the junyi15 prerequisite graph is planned, not run, and conditional on that bed clearing the still-unmeasured exercise-grain positivity bar of Section 4 (Appendix D) [P4] [S25]; the decoupling admission bar itself must be re-certified on this harness rather than inherited [P7]. The design also pre-registers a clean-negative verdict, three named triggers under which the learned-signed claim is reported unsupported, so that failure lands as a stated negative rather than a soft partial verdict [P6]. The hard scope wall around real data is an output of the same discipline. Until the battery runs, no real-data association sentence is licensed, and none appears in this paper.
