## Abstract skeleton (5 sentences, one clause per contribution)

1. [Problem] Deep knowledge-tracing models are read as measurement
   instruments, with growth curves, per-skill mastery, and signed cross-skill
   influence published from their latent states on the strength of prediction
   accuracy alone, which cannot certify any of those readouts.
2. [Contribution 1, protocol] We introduce a certification protocol that
   tests each readout against known ground truth before it is read, using
   matched synthetic twins calibrated to named real datasets, pre-registered
   bars with frozen kill conditions, permutation nulls, designed confound
   arms, and end-to-end seed discipline, with refusals published as
   first-class results.
3. [Contribution 2, growth cycle] The protocol completes one full
   certify-then-confirm cycle: a cohort-grain growth-existence detector is
