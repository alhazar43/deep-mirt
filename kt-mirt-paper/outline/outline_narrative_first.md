## Section 1. Introduction

**Job.** Establish that deep KT latent-state readouts are consumed as
measurement while validated only by prediction, name the cost by
precedent, and state the contribution sentence with four
conjunction-scoped clauses.

Ordered claims and evidence:

1. The field publishes growth curves, mastery states, and cross-skill
   influence graphs from KT latent states, and its nearest neighbors
   validate them by prediction alone. Evidence: S21 (LTKT DOI, HawkesKT
   NDCG .83; sweep found both target claims open); framing memo section
   3 fact-check verdicts (HawkesKT SIGNED but uncertified and
   response-conditioned; LTKT SIGNED but stipulated pre-computed inputs,
   test leakage stated by its authors).
2. Prediction cannot certify a readout; neither can raw learning
   curves, which are attrition-biased and blind to the false-fire

   curve shows this for free" objection (threat table row 5).
3. Paper 1 precedent, one paragraph exactly as the continuity brief
   scopes it: item-side static readouts can be stable and wrong with a
   priced decision cost in simulation; person-side summaries are the
   fragile quantity; this paper extends the gate to person-side
   dynamics and multi-KC structure. Never re-litigate, never imply SK
   fixes person-side readouts. Evidence: continuity brief sections 1
   and 4.
4. Contribution sentence, four clauses (framing memo section 2,

   - C1 protocol (M1-M8, M12, M15; lineage T7-T10 owned preemptively);
   - C2 certified growth-existence readout with one closed

## Section 5. The Growth-Existence Readout: A Completed Cycle

**Job.** Walk the full certify, diagnose, repair, confirm loop for the
one readout that earned it, with the refusals at finer grain presented
as results of equal rank inside the same section.

### 5.1 Certification at cohort grain

1. The passive gate certifies as a twin-level detector on both density
   profiles: silent on every null twin, at the permutation floor on
   every growth twin, surviving a full density inversion (G1);
   null-twin split-half behavior clean at both densities (G2).
2. The raw-statistic separation on KDD is a density artifact and is not
   sold; only the calibrated permutation p-value recovers the
   separation at thin density (G3, killed-refused, reported here as a
   result that disciplines the headline).
3. Strength is capped at the permutation floor; the gate says growth is
   present, never how much (G4).

### 5.2 The saturation false fire, diagnosed and repaired

1. CG6 inverts on both profiles: the gate fires hardest exactly where
   it must stay silent (G5, killed-refused, presented as the harness
   probing reference-model misspecification, threat row 7).
2. Mechanism: no-growth reference approximates a saturating curve
   worse, corroborated by numerical degeneracy (G6); density modulates
   severity, not existence (G7).
3. The saturation-aware bed null repairs the false fire without harming
   detection or the null (G8). Certification is thereafter stated
   relative to the repaired reference.

### 5.3 Refusals at finer grain (results, not caveats)

1. Per-KC growth resolution refused at both density extremes,
   comparable margins, same signature, invariant under density
   inversion; the "more data will not license it" sentence carried as
   labeled inference, never theorem (G9, with G10 the BH-power zero and
   the dependence-sensitivity accounting). Ancestors cited in place:
   Beck & Chang, Khajah. G9 leads because it is the one refusal with
   genuinely new content (threat row 22).
2. Per-KC rate recovery fails far below its bar, the pre-registered K7
   identifiability-floor finding (G11).
3. ACT refused as magnitude estimator and per-KC ranker (G13), with
   the honest remainder stated: presence and direction recovered, thin
   density rank markedly better (G14), but no variant is clean on null
   and growth twins across both profiles simultaneously (G15).
4. The misfit clause is unusable by opposite numeric routes on the two
   profiles (G16).
5. Split-half reliability passes on non-saturated twins and is
   diagnostically blown out on the saturated twin (G19); Tier-2 KC
   rate reliability cannot be certified from any existing artifact, a
   named harness wiring gap, not a statistical null (G20,
   engineering).

### 5.4 The field-default tracker fails the battery

1. PAS-N1 fails the faithfulness battery consistently on both beds at
   production scale, all four audits (G17, killed-refused, headlined
   as the field-default architecture failing, threat row 23); the one
   borderline cell stated (G18).
2. PAS-N2's superiority is a construction guarantee with no measured
   CG7-CG10 verdict in any cell; sanity probe only (P2, pending,
   mandatory wording).

### 5.5 Real data: the fire, the silence, the boundary

1. Synthetic roll-up first: partially certified, with every not-earned
   item listed (G23).
2. The certified detector transfers: fires on real KDD Algebra under
   the saturation-aware null, on the unsaturated 257 of 515 KCs, one
   seed, one cohort split, B=99, p=0.01. Subset and breadth clauses in
   the same sentence as the fire, always (R1; claim-language growth
   pattern). Conditionality on bank fidelity at 0.70-0.80 and the
   mastery-managed ITS ordering confound named here (G12, threat row
   12).
3. R5 co-located: model-free first-attempt curves rise 12-19 points, so
   the fire validates the instrument against a known-growth bed, never
   discovers growth (threat row 1).
4. The designed negative pair: correct silence on thin-practice Junyi,
   random-40k vs biased first-40k ruling out sampling bias, mapping a
   density boundary, not absence of learning (R2). R1 and R2 presented
   as one designed positive/negative real-data pair (threat row 27).
5. The deep-Junyi cell pending, both outcomes drafted neutrally: fire
   confirms the thin-data explanation, silence narrows the interval
   (P1; rule 10).
6. Overall verdict: partially certified, exact wording from R3;
   real-bed reporting licensed only at Tier 1 (R4).

### 6.1 Feasibility and sign recovery

1. CT0 is inconclusive-but-alive: no cell clears the full bar at D=3,
   but the sign-unidentifiability kill does not fire (S1).
2. Per-edge signed coefficients recovered near truth at single-tag
   density, refuting sign-unidentifiability outright (S2).
3. The failure is discrimination, not recovery: true-zero leakage
   overlaps the negative reference dose (S3); the positive half is
   sign-separable from the smallest tested cell with a measured
   effective-sample ladder (S4); the negative half has no stable
   threshold at 1x dose (S5), and the null band grows with epochs in a
   flat-NLL basin, reproducing the ACT-P0 pathology (S6).

### 6.2 The repair that failed, and what that taught

1. The L1-and-early-stopping repair hypothesis is refuted: no
   configuration pins true zeros while sparing true edges; winner rule
   frozen in code before results (S9, killed-refused).
2. CT0's trainer-artifact explanation is overturned: the leak is a
   small-signal identifiability property of the objective at this
   grain (S10, killed-refused). The stationarity re-derivation closes
   with default numbers standing (S20, engineering).

### 6.3 The certified floor and its movers

1. The negative-edge detectable-dose floor is 0.04, twice the
   field-anchored reference dose, measured on clean certification
   seeds under pre-registered per-dose bars, certified on the
   synthetic harness (S11, conservative tag; adjudication footnote
   here). Floor-measurement is the capability; floor-transport is an
   explicitly refused claim.
2. The floor moves, measured: sign recovery collapses outright at
   multi-tag density (S7, killed-refused), and bank error at the
   measured recovery floor degrades without collapsing the positive
   half (S8). Both fold into the map as floor-modifier cells (threat
   row 17). Framed with Hertzog and Card as the first measured point
   of a power surface (threat row 14).
3. The binding constraint is a dose-independent 5-15% true-zero
   false-edge background, so per-edge existence on real data requires
   multiplicity control (S12, conservative tag).

### 6.4 Controls, the kill, and the licensed sentence

1. The phantom-transfer control passes: freeing gamma fabricates
   transfer on the null twin 5/5; the pin retained on structural
   grounds (S13, conservative tag; S17 the qmirt ancestry of the pin;

2. The order-shuffle negative control refutes the temporal-transfer

3. The causal cumulative-dose reading is unearned, not refuted:

4. The licensed sentence, in full: per-edge signed dose-association,

5. Positioning: the three-way conjunction (model-recovered AND

6. What remains unexecuted, stated plainly: the re-baselined full

## Abstract skeleton (5 sentences, one clause per contribution)

1. [Disease] Deep knowledge-tracing models are read as measurement
   instruments, their latent states published as growth curves and
   cross-skill influence graphs, yet the field validates these readouts
   by prediction accuracy, which cannot certify them.
2. [C1, protocol] We present a certification protocol that decides,
