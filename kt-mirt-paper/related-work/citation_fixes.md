## Notes

- Andersen (1985) models the latent correlation between two testing occasions in a Rasch frame; it grounds "Rasch-family longitudinal measurement" cleanly. If the sentence claims growth *trajectories*, Embretson (1991) is the load-bearing cite of the pair.
- Items 5 and 6 form a cited debate pair: Cronbach & Furby (1970) is the pessimistic classic, Rogosa & Willett (1983) the rehabilitation. The draft's phrase "long-running debate over the reliability of difference scores" is accurate only if both sides are cited with their actual positions.
- No citation was found to be fabricated or unresolvable; all eleven exist as described.
