# Claim-evidence map (kt-mirt paper)

Consolidated from ledger, verdict files, design pre-registrations, bed triage, and the plain briefing. Deduplication keeps the most precise statement and best source pointer. Strength tags are never upgraded; where duplicate extractions disagreed, the weaker tag is kept and the conflict is listed at the end. Killed/refused entries are first-class results, not footnotes.

Strength vocabulary: `certified` (passed pre-registered synthetic certification under seed discipline) > `real-data` (measured on a real bed) > `synthetic-only` (synthetic result, not elevated to certification) > `pending` (designed or in flight, no result). `killed-refused` marks refuted hypotheses and refused claims. `engineering` marks implementation and protocol facts.

## Conflicts and doubts

1. **Dose floor and false-edge background (S11, S12): strength conflict.** The primary verdict file and ledger tag the |g|=0.04 floor and the ~5-15% background as `certified` (clean seeds 0-4, untuned default, pre-registered bars); the plain-briefing retelling of the identical result tags it `synthetic-only`. Per the never-upgrade rule the weaker tag is kept. Judgment: the verdict file is authoritative and the seed discipline (M5) supports certification-grade status; the paper can argue the stronger tag but the map records the conflict.
2. **CT6 phantom control (S13): same conflict pattern** — `certified` in ledger and verdict, `synthetic-only` in the briefing. Weaker tag kept.
3. **Tension between S16 and S11.** S16 (signed dose-association certified at D=3) is tagged `certified` on agreement of both its sources, yet its negative-half floor is the same 0.04 measurement downgraded in S11. If the conservative reading is adopted, S16's negative-half clause inherits the S11 caveat.
4. **PAS-N1 (G17) and EdNet-density collapse (S7): tag-category ambiguity.** One extractor tagged these `synthetic-only`, another `killed-refused`. These tags are not ordered; `killed-refused` is kept per the keep-kills-prominent rule, with the scope explicitly synthetic in both claims.
5. **Test-suite counts unreconciled (E1).** The ledger reports growth to 504 tests; the bridge notes report 454 collected items at their snapshot. Different documents, different dates; the discrepancy is a snapshot-ordering question, not a failure signal (0 failed in every reported run).
6. **Superseded rows dropped.** The verdicts' "saturation-aware null is a precondition" (pending) was fulfilled by the implemented fix (G8) and its real-data application (R1). The design-time "13 gates must all pass" and "CT0 gates the matrix" pre-registration rows are superseded by the executed verdicts and folded into M3/M4/S1.
7. **The real KDD fire (R1) is one seed and one cohort split** at B=99. It is the sole real-data positive for the growth detector; replication breadth is the map's thinnest point under a certified headline.
8. **junyi15 growth magnitude (R6) carries an unresolved within-topic difficulty-sequencing confound**; part of the 19-point rise could be easier-exercises-first ordering, unchecked in triage.
9. **Junyi15 graph annotation coverage is uncertain** (~370-742 exercises depending on source; triage measured 742/837 non-empty), and the graph contains cycles — both constrain the planned external check (P4).
10. **Deep-Junyi outcome unknown (P1)** at the time of this map; whichever way it lands the density-boundary interval narrows, but no number exists yet.
11. **Minor numeric drift treated as consistent:** EdNet decoupling 0.867 (triage) vs 0.87 (design, rounded); briefing's "one in ten empty pairs" vs verdict's "~5-15%" background; ledger's "19/20 EdNet cells" vs the verdict's syn_ns seed1-absent accounting; bank recovery "0.70-0.80" vs per-cell 0.718-0.770.
