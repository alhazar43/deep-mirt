## Part 1 — DEFECTS, ranked

**D1. FATAL (headline mechanism claim). The amortization-gap mechanism has zero in-paper evidence; the identifying experiment exists, banked, and was left out.**
The design says the estimator ladder "localize[s] the recovery gap to the amortized item-to-parameter path" (`:811`) and the discussion interprets the refit at length (`:1393-1399`), but no ladder number, table, or figure appears anywhere in `:804-1367` or the appendices. The evidence exists outside the paper: `outputs/p2_oracle/oracle_control_report.txt` (N=2000, GPCM, LSTM): shared readout .719 | two-stage MLE at the model's own theta_hat .934 | oracle clamp at theta* .979 | decoupled readout .941 | mirt full-MML .982; encoder generality in `oracle_control_report_r2_ladder.txt` (transformer shared .438 → two-stage .914). As the paper stands, it reports a *difference* (SH < SK) and asserts a *mechanism* with nothing between them. A reviewer writes: "the central causal claim of the paper is unsupported by any experiment in the paper."

**D2. FATAL as a controlled experiment (headline effect size). The SH-vs-SK contrast is confounded with a 2.6x parameter increase concentrated entirely on the item side, and no capacity-matched control appears in the results.**
Exact counts (from `core/encoder.py:240-250`, `core/decoders.py:194-198`, `_p2_gpcm_alpha_key.py:84`, `_p2_nrm_channels.py:106-113`; totals confirmed by the n_params column of `outputs/p2_width/width_table.md`): at Q=200, GPCM-LSTM SH = 8,101 parameters; SK = 21,125. The delta of 13,024 is the Q×64 key table (12,800) plus head widening (224). Per item: SH gives 8 numbers, SK gives 72, to recover a 4-parameter truth. 2PL: 8,067 vs 20,979. NRM: 8,137 vs 21,385. The paper's own banked controls — listed in tab:hyper (`:1708-1709`) but with no results anywhere — show the confound is material: widening the *shared* embedding alone moves GPCM slope recovery .823 (w8) → .917 (w64) vs decoupled .963 (`outputs/p2_width/width_table.md`, LSTM, realistic bed); the narrow-key control (`outputs/p2_narrowkey`, spiraled bed, N=2000) gets .823 GPCM / .780 2PL with a width-16 key, against SH .719/.553 and SK-64 .941/.898. So roughly half of the headline gap is per-item width, not routing. A genuine routing effect survives at matched capacity (w64-shared .917, 34k params, still loses to decoupled .963 at 21k), but it is 3-5x smaller than what tab:mass displays, and the limitations section concedes the sweep covers one of nine cells (`:1476`). Reviewer: "you compared your weakest shared configuration against your strongest separated one and called the difference structural."

**D3. MAJOR, borderline FATAL for the real-data person-side claims. "SH" denotes different architectures in the synthetic and real studies, and different architectures across columns of the same real table.**
`_p2_realstudy.py:161-177` (`engine_kwargs_for`): real 2PL/GPCM SH = emb_dim 64, no key; real NRM SH = emb_dim 8; synthetic SH = emb 8 everywhere (`_p2_exposure_sweep.py:153`). The paper describes only the width-8 SH (`:846-849`) and never discloses the real-data widths. Consequences: (i) the real SH arm is the *larger* model (~37k params, LSTM input width 128) while SK is ~24k, inverting the synthetic capacity relation — so no cross-study statement about "the SH path" refers to a fixed object; (ii) the person-side real results (EdNet 2PL final ability vs raw score r=.36 SH vs .54 SK, `:1206`; .41/.64 vs EAP, app `:1662`) change two things at once: the readout routing and an 8x wider encoder value input, which by the codebase's own design rationale (`core/encoder.py:10-19`: a fat value "lets the LSTM memorize items into the ability state") independently degrades theta. The claim that the binary view is "a control on the item-to-parameter path" (`:1188`) is false on the person side.

**D4. MAJOR. Optimization parity is mechanical, not demonstrated; the exculpatory evidence exists and is unreported.**
Both arms share one untuned recipe: Adam lr 1e-2, 150 epochs, patience 10 on an inner-val prediction loss (synthetic, `_p2_exposure_sweep.py:248-252`, restore-best halt in `_p2_model.py:273-405`); lr 1e-2, fixed 120 epochs, patience=None (real, `_p2_realstudy.py:110-113,380`). No lr sweep exists anywhere in the artifacts. This matters because SH's discrimination is the slow mode: the banked 500-epoch no-early-stop trajectory study (`docs/exposure_rerun_results.md` Phase 3) shows SH's a-recovery peaks at epochs 125-225 while SK peaks at 50-100 — so a patience-10 stop on prediction loss plausibly truncates SH mid-climb. The same study also *refutes* the under-training alternative: SH's peak (.477-.776 across the four cells) never reaches SK's peak (.774-.949) in any of 12 cells. That is exactly the result a dynamics reviewer would demand — SH loses at its own optimum, at every stopping time — and it is in no table or figure of the paper. Reviewer: "either report the training-time evidence or accept that a slower credit-assignment path plus prediction-keyed early stopping explains your gap."

**D5. MAJOR. Zero misspecification-robustness evidence; every recovery number is matched-family.**
The generator and fitted head are the same family in every synthetic cell (`:815-824`); the 7-violation probe (`kt-irt/src/deep_irt/bench/run_misspecification_probe.py`: local dependence, noisy thresholds, response style, threshold disorder, DIF, exposure imbalance, drifting theta) was built and never run. The paper's whole thrust is that parameters get *used* downstream; the regime where SH-vs-SK ordering could invert (misspecified truth) is untested, and the real-data sections cannot substitute (no ground truth, `:882-888`).

**D6. MAJOR. The EdNet-NRM "structured head beats the direct predictor" result is a baseline artifact by the paper's own numbers.**
Claim: gains of .09-.12 over direct predictors show the NRM head "captures item-option structure that direct option predictors do not recover" (`:1093-1096`). But the item-wise popular-option lookup scores .653 (`:1275-1277`), above every model in the table, and the direct baselines (.526-.565) sit 9-13 points *below* a frequency table despite conditioning on the current item embedding through an MLP head (`_p2_direct_model.py:89-102`) under the identical 120-epoch budget. A direct model that cannot learn per-item marginals is an optimization failure, not a floor; the NRM head's advantage is consistent with nothing deeper than its c_k intercepts being a linear per-item lookup (an easy optimization path to option popularity). The honest top-1 statement is that no model beats item marginals on this slice. The NLL decomposition (`:1307-1315`) is the right instrument but lacks the one row that would settle it: NLL of the per-item multinomial-marginal baseline.

**D7. MAJOR. A pre-registered metric was computed, banked, and dropped.**
The frozen prereg (`docs/real_data_prereg.md`, metric 2) and the driver (`_p2_realstudy.py:26-28,409-412`) define the truth-free slack delta = 1 - Spearman(readout alpha, per-item MLE refit at the model's own theta_hat), persisted for every fold-point in `results/p2_realstudy/*/d*_f*.json`. It appears nowhere in the paper ("slack" has zero matches in `:804-1762`). Under the prereg's own rule ("every cell reported whatever it shows"), this is selective reporting — and it is the *deployable* audit statistic the discussion's decision table (`:1407-1430`) implicitly needs.

**D8. MAJOR (statistics). The real-data prediction table has no uncertainty and mixes incomparable cell sizes; a psychometrically indefensible disattenuation is used.**
tab:real_prediction reports 3-digit accuracies with no intervals and mixes 25-fold cells with 3-fold (DKVMN-direct) and 5-6-fold cells (`:1081-1084`), then draws cross-encoder conclusions from 1-2 point margins (`:1090`). Several EdNet headline quantities are fold-0-of-5-seeds only (`:1302`, app note `:1648`). The disattenuated ability correlations (.59/.63, `:1252`) use cross-seed reproducibility as the reliability coefficient (`:1262-1264`); init-variance is not measurement reliability, and using it inflates the disattenuated values. The synthetic tables, by contrast, are correctly seed-clustered (5 effective clusters, `_p2_cluster_bootstrap.py:5-17`) and honestly flagged descriptive (`:866-870`).

**D9. MINOR-MAJOR. tab:hyper misdescribes the real-data runs.**
It states epochs ≤150 with patience 10 and batch up to 1024 (`:1713-1716`); the real study ran fixed 120 epochs, no early stopping, batch 512 (`_p2_realstudy.py:110-113,380`). A reproduction from the appendix fails.

**D10. MINOR. "Prediction changes remain small" (fig:dd caption, `:995`) is false for the polytomous transformer cells.**
Transf-GPCM .459→.492 and Transf-NRM .449→.489 (tab:mass) are 3-4 point gains on ~.46 baselines. In those cells recovery and accuracy co-move — the pattern plain capacity relief predicts — which narrows the clean dissociation evidence to the 2PL rows.

**D11. MINOR. Promised boundary results are absent, and the banked boundary result reverses the headline.**
Boundary runs N=50-5000 are promised (`:824`, tab:beds `:917-919`); nothing appears. The banked crossover (`docs/exposure_rerun_results.md` Phase 4a, `outputs/p2_crossover`) shows SH *wins* at E=15 for 2PL (18/25 paired folds), with E* in (15,30]. Calling thin-exposure regions "unresolved" (`:1452-1454`) understates a resolved sign reversal. Related: the discussion's CAT direction ("SK reduces these costs," `:1386-1388`) is contradicted at fixed length by the one banked real-bank CAT in the keep-set (`results/p2_cat_realbank/realbank_cat.md`: excess miscls@20 SH-raw +.066 vs SK-raw +.075).

**D12. MINOR. fig:scatter is one hand-picked fit (`:1009`), and the nominal panels' common rescale is fixed by the separated fit (`:1012-1015`), which favors SK visually; rank claims unaffected.**

**Board answers, condensed.** (a) Counts above; no capacity-matched control exists in the paper; banked width/key sweeps attribute roughly half the gap to width. (b) Parity is mechanical (same recipe both arms), tuned on neither; the banked 500-epoch study cleanly separates under-training from structure — in the paper's favor — but is unreported; SH's late a-peak vs prediction-keyed early stopping is a live worry the paper never addresses. (c) The frozen-state refit exists (`outputs/p2_oracle`, incl. `refit_separate_report.txt`); it proves the encoder states retain the information and the deficit is item-side, but it bypasses the item embedding *and* the head jointly, so it cannot distinguish corrupted embedding from mis-set map. (d) Nothing in the results distinguishes the amortization story from garden-variety multi-task gradient interference on the shared embedding (conflicting gradients from the theta-formation path and the low-Fisher alpha readout — the standard prediction of gradient-surgery-era theory); the NRM routing fix is itself interference evidence on the person side, making interference the more parsimonious unified account the paper never tests against.

## Part 2 — WHAT SURVIVES

1. **A prediction-recovery dissociation exists (existence proof, 2PL cells).** At the reference cohort, accuracy ties within .006 while discrimination rank recovery differs by .34-.43 (tab:mass rows 957-959; 18/18 recovery deltas positive across the grid). Same fits verified in the artifact store. This survives regardless of the capacity confound, because it is a statement about *observational equivalence*, not about cause: two models indistinguishable in next-response accuracy carry very different item parameters. This is the paper's strongest defensible fact.
2. **Adding a decoupled wide item key improves item-parameter rank recovery at matched training budget, direction robust across 3 encoders x 3 heads x 4 cohort sizes** (tab:mass, tab:massfull; alpha/a_k +.15 to +.46, beta/c_k +.09 to +.35, seed-clustered CIs mostly disjoint). Honest attribution: an architecture change worth ~2.6x parameters, of which a genuine routing component of roughly +.05 (GPCM, vs w64-shared) to +.12 (2PL) survives at matched or superior shared capacity in the banked controls.
3. **Scale does not close the 2PL shared deficit within this family**: N=5000, LSTM .613 vs .946 (tab:massfull `:1559`). Matched-family synthetic only.
4. **IRT heads are prediction-competitive on binary/ordered real data** (within ~1 point of DKT-style direct heads; TIMSS clustered at .58), and the TIMSS ordinal structure is path-stable: 31/31 items ordered thresholds under both designs with near-identical locations (app `:1607-1637`). Modest and sound.
5. **The real EdNet 2PL item map is path-insensitive** (SH-SK Spearman .998 beta, .978 alpha, `:1191`) — a genuinely informative negative result: on real binary data the item-side readout does not care about the path, which sharpens where the phenomenon lives (low-Fisher parameter groups, person side, nominal slopes).

## Part 3 — THE ONE DEMAND

**A single pre-registered capacity-x-routing factorial with the ladder and a frozen-embedding rung attached, at the reference cohort.** This one bed simultaneously settles D1, D2, D4, and the interference question (d); everything needed already exists in `_p2_gpcm_alpha_key.py`, the width-sweep bed, and `_p2_oracle_control.py`.

Design, LSTM x {2PL, GPCM, NRM}, spiraled bed N=2000/Q=200, 5 seeds x 5 folds, the exact TrainConfig of the paper:

- **Arms (per decoder):** SH-w8 (paper's SH); SH-w72 (shared value width 72 — *exact* per-item match to SK's 8+64, total params match to within ~200); SK-8+64 (paper's SK); SK-8+8 (separate key of width 8 — routing at matched thin width, the pure-routing control); each also run to 400 epochs without early stopping, recovery checkpointed every 25.
- **Rungs on every arm:** two-stage per-item refit at own theta_hat, and the new discriminator — **freeze the trained SH encoder and item-value table, train a fresh readout head (and separately a fresh 64-wide key table) on the frozen model**. If a fresh head on frozen SH embeddings recovers alpha, the amortized map was mis-set (amortization story); if it stays low but a fresh *key table* recovers, the embedding itself lost the information to interference. Log, during one fit per arm, the batch cosine between the prediction-loss gradient and the alpha-readout gradient on the shared embedding: a persistently negative or near-orthogonal-with-large-norm-ratio signal is direct evidence for the interference account.
- **Pre-registered contrasts:** (i) SK-8+64 vs SH-w72 = the routing effect at matched capacity (my expectation from the banked sweeps: +.04 to +.06 GPCM, ~+.05 2PL — report *this* as the headline, not +.22/+.35); (ii) SK-8+8 vs SH-w8 = routing at matched width; (iii) refit rungs = the mechanism localization currently missing from the paper.

Roughly 150 fits at ~25k parameters each — smaller than one toggle slice. If (i) comes out at +.05 with disjoint clustered CIs and the frozen-embedding rung resolves the mechanism, the paper has a true, smaller, *identified* result and survives review. Without it, the headline numbers measure an architecture upgrade sold as a structural diagnosis.
